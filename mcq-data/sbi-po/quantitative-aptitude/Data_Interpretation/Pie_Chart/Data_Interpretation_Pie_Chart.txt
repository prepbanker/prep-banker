Directions (Q1–Q5): Refer to diagram: SVG/Q1.svg.
The pie chart shows the percentage distribution of 15,000 students enrolled across 5 different schools (A, B, C, D, E).

Q1. What is the total number of students enrolled in School C?
(A) 4000
(B) 4200
(C) 4500
(D) 4800

Answer: C
Explanation:
School C = 30% of Total.
Total students = 15,000.
30% of 15000 = (30/100) × 15000 = 4500.

----------------------------------------

Q2. What is the difference between the number of students in School A and School D?
(A) 700
(B) 750
(C) 800
(D) 850

Answer: B
Explanation:
School A = 25%. School D = 20%.
Difference in % = 25% - 20% = 5%.
Difference in number = 5% of 15000 = (5/100) × 15000 = 750.

----------------------------------------

Q3. The number of students in School B is what percentage of the number of students in School E?
(A) 120%
(B) 125%
(C) 150%
(D) 175%

Answer: C
Explanation:
School B = 15%.
School E = 10%.
Percentage = (15 / 10) × 100 = 150%.

----------------------------------------

Q4. If 40% of the students in School A are females, what is the number of male students in School A?
(A) 2250
(B) 2150
(C) 2350
(D) 2450

Answer: A
Explanation:
Total students in A = 25% of 15000 = 3750.
Male students = (100% - 40%) = 60%.
60% of 3750 = 0.6 × 3750 = 2250.

----------------------------------------

Q5. What is the ratio of the number of students in Schools C and D together to the number of students in Schools B and E together?
(A) 2 : 1
(B) 3 : 1
(C) 4 : 3
(D) 5 : 2

Answer: A
Explanation:
Schools (C + D) % = 30% + 20% = 50%.
Schools (B + E) % = 15% + 10% = 25%.
Ratio = 50% : 25% = 2 : 1.

Directions (Q6–Q10): Refer to diagram: SVG/Q6.svg.
The pie chart gives the distribution of a family's monthly income (Rs. 60,000) across various categories. The distribution is given in terms of central angles (in degrees).

Q6. What is the amount spent by the family on Food?
(A) Rs. 16,000
(B) Rs. 18,000
(C) Rs. 20,000
(D) Rs. 22,000

Answer: B
Explanation:
Central angle for Food = 108°.
Amount = (108° / 360°) × 60000 = (3 / 10) × 60000 = Rs. 18,000.

----------------------------------------

Q7. The amount saved by the family is what percentage of the amount spent on Education?
(A) 120%
(B) 133.33%
(C) 125%
(D) 150%

Answer: B
Explanation:
Central angle for Savings = 72°.
Central angle for Education = 54°.
Percentage = (72 / 54) × 100 = (4 / 3) × 100 = 133.33%.

----------------------------------------

Q8. How much more does the family spend on Rent than on Miscellaneous expenses?
(A) Rs. 3,000
(B) Rs. 2,500
(C) Rs. 3,500
(D) Rs. 4,000

Answer: A
Explanation:
Angle for Rent = 72°.
Angle for Misc = 54°.
Difference in angle = 72° - 54° = 18°.
Difference in amount = (18 / 360) × 60000 = (1 / 20) × 60000 = Rs. 3,000.

----------------------------------------

Q9. What is the total amount spent on Food and Rent together?
(A) Rs. 32,000
(B) Rs. 28,000
(C) Rs. 30,000
(D) Rs. 36,000

Answer: C
Explanation:
Total angle = 108° (Food) + 72° (Rent) = 180°.
Since 180° is exactly half of a circle (360°),
Amount = 50% of 60000 = Rs. 30,000.

----------------------------------------

Q10. If the income of the family increases by 20% in the next month and the central angle for Savings remains the same, what will be the new amount saved?
(A) Rs. 15,400
(B) Rs. 14,400
(C) Rs. 12,000
(D) Rs. 16,800

Answer: B
Explanation:
New Income = 60000 × 1.20 = 72000.
Savings angle = 72° (which is 72/360 = 20% of the total).
New Savings = 20% of 72000 = Rs. 14,400.

Directions (Q11–Q15): Refer to diagram: SVG/Q11.svg.
The pie chart provides the demographic breakdown (by age groups) of a town's registered voters (Total = 24,000).

Q11. What is the total number of registered voters in the age group of 36 - 45 years?
(A) 4200
(B) 4800
(C) 5000
(D) 4600

Answer: B
Explanation:
Age 36-45 = 20%.
Voters = 20% of 24000 = 4800.

----------------------------------------

Q12. The number of voters in the 18 - 25 years group is what percentage more than the number of voters in the 26 - 35 years group?
(A) 40%
(B) 35%
(C) 25%
(D) 30%

Answer: A
Explanation:
18 - 25 = 35%.
26 - 35 = 25%.
Difference = 10%.
Percentage more = (10 / 25) × 100 = 40%.

----------------------------------------

Q13. What is the central angle for the sector representing voters aged 56+ years?
(A) 26.8°
(B) 28.8°
(C) 32.4°
(D) 24.5°

Answer: B
Explanation:
56+ years = 8%.
Central angle = (8 / 100) × 360° = 28.8°.

----------------------------------------

Q14. What is the total number of voters who are 36 years old or above?
(A) 9600
(B) 8400
(C) 9200
(D) 8800

Answer: A
Explanation:
Groups 36-45 (20%), 46-55 (12%), and 56+ (8%).
Total percentage = 20 + 12 + 8 = 40%.
Number of voters = 40% of 24000 = 9600.

----------------------------------------

Q15. If 40% of the voters in the 18-25 age group and 60% of the voters in the 26-35 age group cast their votes, what is the total number of votes cast by these two age groups combined?
(A) 6960
(B) 6840
(C) 6720
(D) 7080

Answer: A
Explanation:
18-25 group = 35% of 24000 = 8400.
Votes cast = 40% of 8400 = 3360.
26-35 group = 25% of 24000 = 6000.
Votes cast = 60% of 6000 = 3600.
Total votes = 3360 + 3600 = 6960.
