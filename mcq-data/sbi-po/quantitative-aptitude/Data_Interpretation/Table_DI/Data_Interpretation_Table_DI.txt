Directions (Q1–Q5): Refer to diagram: SVG/Q2.svg.
The table gives the number of different items (Laptops, Mobiles, Tablets, Smartwatches) sold by 5 different stores (A, B, C, D, E) in a month.

Q1. What is the total number of items sold by Store C?
(A) 715
(B) 685
(C) 745
(D) 725

Answer: A
Explanation:
Total items sold by C = 90 (Laptops) + 380 (Mobiles) + 160 (Tablets) + 85 (Smartwatches)
= 90 + 380 + 160 + 85 = 715.

----------------------------------------

Q2. What is the ratio of the number of Mobiles sold by Store A to the number of Laptops sold by Store E?
(A) 5 : 2
(B) 2 : 5
(C) 7 : 3
(D) 3 : 7

Answer: A
Explanation:
Mobiles sold by A = 450
Laptops sold by E = 180
Ratio = 450 : 180 = 45 : 18 = 5 : 2.

----------------------------------------

Q3. The number of Tablets sold by Store D is approximately what percentage of the total number of items sold by Store D?
(A) 24%
(B) 20%
(C) 22%
(D) 26%

Answer: B
Explanation:
Total items sold by D = 210 + 610 + 240 + 140 = 1200
Tablets sold by D = 240
Percentage = (240 / 1200) × 100 = 20%.

----------------------------------------

Q4. What is the difference between the average number of Mobiles sold by all stores and the average number of Smartwatches sold by all stores?
(A) 390
(B) 380
(C) 375
(D) 385

Answer: B
Explanation:
Total Mobiles = 450 + 520 + 380 + 610 + 490 = 2450
Average Mobiles = 2450 / 5 = 490
Total Smartwatches = 90 + 110 + 85 + 140 + 125 = 550
Average Smartwatches = 550 / 5 = 110
Difference = 490 - 110 = 380.

Q5. The total number of items sold by Store B is what percentage more than the total number of items sold by Store A?
(A) 15%
(B) 16.66%
(C) 20%
(D) 12.5%

Answer: B
Explanation:
Total items by B = 150 + 520 + 200 + 110 = 980
Total items by A = 120 + 450 + 180 + 90 = 840
Difference = 980 - 840 = 140
Percentage more = (140 / 840) × 100 = (1/6) × 100 = 16.66%.

Q6. Find the total number of Female students in University Q and S together.
(A) 6180
(B) 6080
(C) 6280
(D) 6380

Answer: A
Explanation:
University Q: Total = 6000, Males = 45%, Females = 55%.

University S: Total = 4800, Males = 40%, Females = 60%.
Females in S = 60% of 4800 = 2880.
Total Females = 3300 + 2880 = 6180.

Q7. In University R, the ratio of Male to Female students in the Science stream is 7 : 6. Find the number of Female students in the Arts stream in University R.
(A) 820
(B) 840
(C) 880
(D) 920

Answer: C
Explanation:
University R: Total = 5200.
Total Males = 60% of 5200 = 3120. Total Females = 40% of 5200 = 2080.
Science students = 50% of 5200 = 2600.
Science Males : Science Females = 7 : 6 (Total 13 units).
Science Females = (6/13) × 2600 = 1200.
Total Females = Science Females + Arts Females.
2080 = 1200 + Arts Females.
Arts Females = 2080 - 1200 = 880.

Q8. What is the central angle corresponding to the Science students of University P if a pie chart is drawn for the total students of University P?
(A) 120°
(B) 135°
(C) 144°
(D) 150°

Explanation:
In University P, Science students constitute 40% of the total.
Central angle = 40% of 360°
