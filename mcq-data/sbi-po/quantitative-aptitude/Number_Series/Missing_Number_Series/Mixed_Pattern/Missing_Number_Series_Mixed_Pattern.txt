Directions (Q1–Q15): What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?

Q1. 5, 11, 24, 51, 106, ?
(A) 217
(B) 215
(C) 212
(D) 220

Answer: A
Explanation:
The pattern is (× 2 + 1), (× 2 + 2), (× 2 + 3)...
5 × 2 + 1 = 11
11 × 2 + 2 = 24
24 × 2 + 3 = 51
51 × 2 + 4 = 106
106 × 2 + 5 = 217.

Q2. 8, 7, 12, 33, 128, ?
(A) 635
(B) 645
(C) 625
(D) 615

Answer: A
Explanation:
The pattern is (× 1 - 1), (× 2 - 2), (× 3 - 3)...
8 × 1 - 1 = 7
7 × 2 - 2 = 12
12 × 3 - 3 = 33
33 × 4 - 4 = 128
128 × 5 - 5 = 640 - 5 = 635.

Q14. 3, 5, 14, 55, 274, ?
(A) 1639
(B) 1649
(C) 1659
(D) 1629

Answer: A
Explanation:
The pattern is (× 1 + 2), (× 2 + 4), (× 3 + 13)... No.
Let's check (× 2 - 1), (× 3 - 1), (× 4 - 1)...
3 × 2 - 1 = 5
5 × 3 - 1 = 14
14 × 4 - 1 = 55
55 × 5 - 1 = 274
274 × 6 - 1 = 1644 - 1 = 1643.
Wait, none of the options is 1643. Let's adjust option A to 1643.

Q14. 3, 5, 14, 55, 274, ?
(A) 1643
(B) 1649
(C) 1659
(D) 1629

Answer: A
Explanation:
The pattern is multiplying by consecutively increasing integers (2, 3, 4...) and subtracting 1 at each step.
3 × 2 - 1 = 5
5 × 3 - 1 = 14
14 × 4 - 1 = 55
55 × 5 - 1 = 274
Next: 274 × 6 - 1 = 1644 - 1 = 1643.
