Directions (Q1–Q15): What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?

Q1. 4, 9, 16, 25, ?
(A) 36
(B) 49
(C) 30
(D) 42

Answer: A
Explanation:
The series consists of consecutive perfect squares: 2², 3², 4², 5².
Next is 6² = 36.

----------------------------------------

Q2. 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ?
(A) 35
(B) 37
(C) 36
(D) 39

Answer: B
Explanation:
The pattern is (n² + 1).
1² + 1 = 2
2² + 1 = 5
3² + 1 = 10
4² + 1 = 17
5² + 1 = 26
Next is 6² + 1 = 37.

----------------------------------------

Q3. 0, 3, 8, 15, 24, ?
(A) 35
(B) 36
(C) 33
(D) 39

Answer: A
Explanation:
The pattern is (n² - 1).
1² - 1 = 0
2² - 1 = 3
3² - 1 = 8
4² - 1 = 15
5² - 1 = 24
Next is 6² - 1 = 35.

----------------------------------------

Q4. 12, 13, 17, 26, 42, ?
(A) 67
(B) 65
(C) 69
(D) 72

Answer: A
Explanation:
Differences are perfect squares.
13 - 12 = 1 (1²)
17 - 13 = 4 (2²)
26 - 17 = 9 (3²)
42 - 26 = 16 (4²)
Next difference is 25 (5²).
42 + 25 = 67.

Q9. 5, 6, 10, 19, 35, ?
(A) 60
(B) 62
(C) 65
(D) 68

Answer: A
Explanation:
Differences are consecutive squares:
6 - 5 = 1 (1²)
10 - 6 = 4 (2²)
19 - 10 = 9 (3²)
35 - 19 = 16 (4²)
Next difference is 25 (5²).
35 + 25 = 60.

Q10. 120, 142, 166, 192, 220, ?
(A) 248
(B) 250
(C) 252
(D) 254

Answer: B
Explanation:
The numbers are (n² + n) starting from n = 10.
Wait, 10² + 10 = 110.
11² + 11 = 121 + 11 = 132.
Let's check the given numbers:
11² - 1 = 120
12² - 2 = 142
13² - 3 = 166
14² - 4 = 192
15² - 5 = 220
Next is 16² - 6 = 256 - 6 = 250.

Q11. 223, 224, 216, 225, 161, ?
(A) 186
(B) 176
(C) 196
(D) 182

Answer: A
Explanation:
Let's check alternating addition/subtraction of something.
224 - 223 = +1 (1²)
216 - 224 = -8 (2³)
225 - 216 = +9 (3²)
161 - 225 = -64 (4³)
Next difference should be +25 (5²).
161 + 25 = 186.
(This is a mixed square/cube pattern, valid for hard level).

Q13. 4, 18, 48, 100, 180, ?
(A) 294
(B) 280
(C) 300
(D) 288

Answer: A
Explanation:
The pattern is (n³ - n²).
2³ - 2² = 8 - 4 = 4
3³ - 3² = 27 - 9 = 18
4³ - 4² = 64 - 16 = 48
5³ - 5² = 125 - 25 = 100
6³ - 6² = 216 - 36 = 180
Next is 7³ - 7² = 343 - 49 = 294.

Q14. 11, 20, 36, 61, 97, ?
(A) 146
(B) 150
(C) 144
(D) 148

Answer: A
Explanation:
Differences are perfect squares:
20 - 11 = 9 (3²)
36 - 20 = 16 (4²)
61 - 36 = 25 (5²)
97 - 61 = 36 (6²)
Next difference is 49 (7²).
97 + 49 = 146.

Q15. 13, 17, 33, 97, ?
(A) 353
(B) 343
(C) 363
(D) 333

Answer: A
Explanation:
Differences:
17 - 13 = 4 (2²)
33 - 17 = 16 (4²)
97 - 33 = 64 (8²)
The square bases double each time: 2, 4, 8.
Next difference will be 16² = 256.
97 + 256 = 353.
