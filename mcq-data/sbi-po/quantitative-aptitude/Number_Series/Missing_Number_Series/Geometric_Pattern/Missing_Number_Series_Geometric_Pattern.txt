Directions (Q1–Q15): What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?

Q1. 4, 12, 36, 108, ?
(A) 324
(B) 316
(C) 304
(D) 342

Answer: A
Explanation:
The pattern is a constant multiplication by 3.
4 × 3 = 12
12 × 3 = 36
36 × 3 = 108
108 × 3 = 324.

----------------------------------------

Q2. 2000, 1000, 500, 250, ?
(A) 100
(B) 150
(C) 125
(D) 75

Answer: C
Explanation:
The pattern is a constant division by 2 (or multiplication by 0.5).
250 ÷ 2 = 125.

----------------------------------------

Q3. 3, 6, 18, 72, ?
(A) 360
(B) 340
(C) 288
(D) 400

Answer: A
Explanation:
The multiplication factor is increasing sequentially.
3 × 2 = 6
6 × 3 = 18
18 × 4 = 72
72 × 5 = 360.

----------------------------------------

Q4. 8, 4, 4, 6, 12, ?
(A) 24
(B) 30
(C) 36
(D) 42

Answer: B
Explanation:
The series multiplies by increasing decimal increments.
8 × 0.5 = 4
4 × 1 = 4
4 × 1.5 = 6
6 × 2 = 12
12 × 2.5 = 30.

----------------------------------------

Q5. 2, 4, 16, 128, ?
(A) 1024
(B) 2048
(C) 512
(D) 256

Answer: B
Explanation:
Multiplication factor doubles at each step.
2 × 2 = 4
4 × 4 = 16
16 × 8 = 128
128 × 16 = 2048.

----------------------------------------

Q6. 6400, 1600, 800, 200, 100, ?
(A) 50
(B) 75
(C) 25
(D) 10

Answer: C
Explanation:
The series alternates between dividing by 4 and dividing by 2.
6400 ÷ 4 = 1600
1600 ÷ 2 = 800
800 ÷ 4 = 200
200 ÷ 2 = 100
100 ÷ 4 = 25.

----------------------------------------

Q7. 5, 7.5, 18.75, 65.625, ?
(A) 265.5
(B) 295.3125
(C) 328.125
(D) 280.25

Answer: B
Explanation:
The multiplication factor increases by 1 each time.
5 × 1.5 = 7.5
7.5 × 2.5 = 18.75
18.75 × 3.5 = 65.625
65.625 × 4.5 = 295.3125.

Q8. 12, 12, 18, 36, 90, ?
(A) 270
(B) 250
(C) 315
(D) 225

Answer: A
Explanation:
The pattern is multiplying by increasing factors of +0.5.
12 × 1 = 12
12 × 1.5 = 18
18 × 2 = 36
36 × 2.5 = 90
90 × 3 = 270.

Q12. 9, 5, 6, 10.5, 23, ?
(A) 55
(B) 60
(C) 64
(D) 72

Answer: B
Explanation:
Pattern: Multiply and add the same factor.
9 × 0.5 + 0.5 = 5
5 × 1 + 1 = 6
6 × 1.5 + 1.5 = 10.5
10.5 × 2 + 2 = 23
23 × 2.5 + 2.5 = 57.5 + 2.5 = 60.

Q13. 7, 4, 5, 9, 20, ?

(D) 55

Answer: B
Explanation:
Pattern: Multiply and add the same factor.

9 × 2 + 2 = 20
20 × 2.5 + 2.5 = 50 + 2.5 = 52.5.

Q14. 8, 5, 7, 13.5, 31, ?
(A) 82.5
(B) 80
(C) 85
(D) 87.5

Answer: A
Explanation:
Pattern: Multiply and add increasing factors.
Wait, let's test:
8 × 0.5 + 1 = 5
5 × 1 + 2 = 7
7 × 1.5 + 3 = 13.5
13.5 × 2 + 4 = 31
31 × 2.5 + 5 = 77.5 + 5 = 82.5.

Q15. 14, 8, 9, 14.5, 30, ?
(A) 76
(B) 72
(C) 80
(D) 85

Answer: A
Explanation:
Pattern: Multiply and add integer increments.
14 × 0.5 + 1 = 8
8 × 1 + 1 = 9 (Wait, increment should change).
Let's re-test: 8 × 1 + 1 = 9.
9 × 1.5 + 1 = 14.5
14.5 × 2 + 1 = 30.
Ah, the pattern is: Multiply by 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2 and always add 1.
30 × 2.5 + 1 = 75 + 1 = 76.
