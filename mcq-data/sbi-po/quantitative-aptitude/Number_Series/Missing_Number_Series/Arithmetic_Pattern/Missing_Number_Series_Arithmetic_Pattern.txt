Directions (Q1–Q15): What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?

Q1. 15, 20, 25, 30, ?
(A) 35
(B) 40
(C) 45
(D) 32

Answer: A
Explanation:
The pattern is a constant addition of +5.
30 + 5 = 35.

Q2. 82, 75, 68, 61, ?
(A) 53
(B) 54
(C) 55
(D) 56

Answer: B
Explanation:

Q7. 100, 98, 93, 85, 74, ?

Q8. 15, 16, 20, 29, 45, ?
(A) 70
(B) 65
(C) 75
(D) 80

Answer: A
Explanation:
Differences:
16 - 15 = 1 (1²)
20 - 16 = 4 (2²)
29 - 20 = 9 (3²)
45 - 29 = 16 (4²)
Next difference = 25 (5²).
45 + 25 = 70.

Q10. 18, 20, 26, 38, 58, ?
(A) 84
(B) 86
(C) 88
(D) 90

Answer: C
Explanation:
Differences:
20 - 18 = +2
26 - 20 = +6
38 - 26 = +12
58 - 38 = +20
Second differences (6-2=4, 12-6=6, 20-12=8).
The second differences are 4, 6, 8, 10.
So next first difference = 20 + 10 = 30.
58 + 30 = 88.

Q11. 12, 23, 45, 80, 130, ?
(A) 197
(B) 195
(C) 205
(D) 187

Answer: A
Explanation:
Differences:
23 - 12 = 11
45 - 23 = 22
80 - 45 = 35
130 - 80 = 50
Second differences:
22 - 11 = 11
35 - 22 = 13
50 - 35 = 15
Next second difference = 17.
Next first difference = 50 + 17 = 67.
130 + 67 = 197.
