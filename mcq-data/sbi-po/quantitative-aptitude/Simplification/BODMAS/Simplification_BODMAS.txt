Directions (Q1–Q15): What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following equations?

Q1. 24 ÷ 6 × 2 + 15 - 3 = ?
(A) 16
(B) 20
(C) 24
(D) 12

Answer: B
Explanation:
According to BODMAS, division comes first: 24 ÷ 6 = 4.
Then multiplication: 4 × 2 = 8.
Then addition: 8 + 15 = 23.
Then subtraction: 23 - 3 = 20.

----------------------------------------

Q2. 45 - [38 - {60 ÷ 3 - (6 - 9 ÷ 3) ÷ 3}] = ?
(A) 21
(B) 24
(C) 26
(D) 27

Answer: C
Explanation:
Solve innermost bracket first: (6 - 9 ÷ 3) = (6 - 3) = 3.
Now equation is: 45 - [38 - {60 ÷ 3 - 3 ÷ 3}]
Solve curly braces: {20 - 1} = 19.
Now equation is: 45 - [38 - 19]
Solve square brackets: [19].
Final step: 45 - 19 = 26.

Q3. 72 ÷ 9 of 2 + 14 = ?
(A) 22
(B) 18
(C) 28
(D) 12

Answer: B
Explanation:
'Of' is solved before division.
9 of 2 = 18.
Then division: 72 ÷ 18 = 4.

Q14. 35% of 160 + ?% of 180 = 50% of 310
(A) 45
(B) 60
(C) 55
(D) 65

Answer: C
Explanation:
35% of 160 = (35/100) × 160 = 56.
50% of 310 = 155.
Equation: 56 + (?% of 180) = 155.
(? / 100) × 180 = 155 - 56 = 99.
? × 1.8 = 99.
? = 99 ÷ 1.8 = 55.
