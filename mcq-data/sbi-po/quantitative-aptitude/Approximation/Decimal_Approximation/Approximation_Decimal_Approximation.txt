Directions (Q1–Q15): What approximate value will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following equations? (You are not expected to calculate the exact value).

Q1. 14.98 × 12.01 + 24.89 = ?
(A) 205
(B) 235
(C) 185
(D) 190

Answer: A
Explanation:
Approximating the values to nearest whole numbers:
15 × 12 + 25
= 180 + 25 = 205.
