Directions (Q1–Q5): Study the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
A family consists of six members: P, Q, R, S, T, and U. There are two married couples in the family.
P is the father of R and the grandfather of U.
Q is the mother-in-law of S.
S is the mother of T.
T is the sister of U.
U is a male.
R is a male.

Q1. How is P related to T?
(A) Father
(B) Grandfather
(C) Uncle
(D) Brother

Answer: B
Explanation:
P is the father of R, and R is married to S (since Q is S's mother-in-law, Q and P are a couple). S is the mother of T. Therefore, R is the father of T, making P the grandfather of T.

----------------------------------------

Q2. How is S related to Q?
(A) Daughter
(B) Sister-in-law
(C) Daughter-in-law
(D) Niece

Answer: C
Explanation:
The statement explicitly says "Q is the mother-in-law of S". This means S is the daughter-in-law of Q.

----------------------------------------

Q3. How is R related to U?
(A) Brother
(B) Uncle
(C) Grandfather
(D) Father

Answer: D
Explanation:
S is the mother of T, and T is the sister of U. This means S is the mother of U. Since R is married to S, R is the father of U.

----------------------------------------

Q4. How many male members are there in the family?
(A) 2
(B) 3
(C) 4
(D) Cannot be determined

Refer to diagram: SVG/Q4.svg

Answer: B
Explanation:
The male members are:
P (father of R),
R (male, explicitly stated),
U (male, explicitly stated).
Thus, there are 3 male members in the family.

----------------------------------------

Q5. Who among the following is the spouse of P?
(A) S
(B) R
(C) Q
(D) T

Answer: C
Explanation:
Since P is the father of R, and Q is the mother-in-law of R's wife (S), Q must be the mother of R. Therefore, P and Q are a married couple, making Q the spouse of P.

Directions (Q11–Q15): Study the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
In a family of 9 members, there are three generations and three married couples.
U is the father of S.
Q is the only sister of S.
R is the brother-in-law of T.
P is the mother-in-law of V.
O is the nephew of V.
M is the granddaughter of U.
T does not have any siblings and is a female.
V is a male.
S has only two children, and O is not a female.

Q11. How is P related to R?
(A) Grandmother
(B) Mother
(C) Aunt
(D) Sister

Answer: B
Explanation:
U is the father of S, and Q is the only sister of S. Since P is the mother-in-law of V (who is a male), and Q is the only sister, V must be married to Q. This makes P the wife of U. R is the brother-in-law of T (who is married to S), meaning R is S's brother. Since P is the mother of S, Q, and R, P is the mother of R.

----------------------------------------

Q12. How is M related to Q?
(A) Daughter
(B) Sister
(C) Niece
(D) Cousin

Answer: C
Explanation:
M is the granddaughter of U and child of S. Q is the sister of S. Therefore, M is the niece of Q.

----------------------------------------

Q13. How many male members are there in the family?
(A) 4
(B) 5
(C) 6
(D) Cannot be determined

Answer: B
Explanation:
The male members are:
U (father of S),
S (husband of T),
R (brother of S),
V (husband of Q),
O (nephew of V, explicitly stated as not a female).
Thus, there are 5 male members.

----------------------------------------

Q14. How is R related to O?
(A) Maternal Uncle
(B) Paternal Uncle
(C) Father
(D) Brother

Refer to diagram: SVG/Q14.svg

Answer: B
Explanation:
R is the brother of S, and S is the father of O. Therefore, R is the paternal uncle of O.

----------------------------------------

Q15. Who among the following is the spouse of V?
(A) T
(B) P
(C) Q
(D) M

Answer: C
Explanation:
P is the mother-in-law of V. V is a male. S, Q, and R are the children of P. Since T is married to S and R is a male, V must be married to the daughter Q. Thus, Q is the spouse of V.
