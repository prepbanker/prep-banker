Directions (Q1–Q15): Arrange the following words in a meaningful and logical sequence.

Q1. 1. Seed  2. Fruit  3. Plant  4. Tree  5. Flower
(A) 1, 3, 4, 5, 2
(B) 1, 4, 3, 5, 2
(C) 1, 3, 5, 4, 2
(D) 3, 1, 4, 5, 2

Answer: A
Explanation:
The logical progression of growth is: 
Seed (1) germinates into a Plant (3). 
The Plant grows into a Tree (4). 
The Tree bears Flowers (5). 
The Flowers turn into Fruits (2).
Correct sequence: 1, 3, 4, 5, 2.

Q2. 1. Word  2. Paragraph  3. Sentence  4. Letter  5. Chapter
(A) 4, 1, 3, 2, 5
(B) 4, 1, 2, 3, 5
(C) 1, 4, 3, 2, 5
(D) 4, 3, 1, 2, 5

Answer: A
Explanation:
The sequence from smallest unit to largest is:
Letter (4) -> Word (1) -> Sentence (3) -> Paragraph (2) -> Chapter (5).

Q3. 1. Key  2. Door  3. Lock  4. Room  5. Switch on
(A) 1, 2, 3, 4, 5
(B) 1, 3, 2, 4, 5
(C) 1, 2, 4, 3, 5
(D) 1, 4, 2, 3, 5

Answer: B
Explanation:
To enter a room and turn on the light:
First take the Key (1), put it in the Lock (3), open the Door (2), enter the Room (4), and Switch on (5) the lights.
Correct sequence: 1, 3, 2, 4, 5.

Q4. 1. Police  2. Punishment  3. Crime  4. Judge  5. Judgement
(A) 3, 1, 2, 4, 5
(B) 3, 1, 4, 5, 2
(C) 1, 3, 4, 5, 2
(D) 3, 4, 1, 5, 2

Answer: B
Explanation:
First, a Crime (3) is committed. Then the Police (1) investigate. The accused is presented before a Judge (4). The judge gives a Judgement (5), which leads to Punishment (2).
Correct sequence: 3, 1, 4, 5, 2.

Q5. 1. Poverty  2. Population  3. Death  4. Unemployment  5. Disease
(A) 2, 4, 1, 5, 3
(B) 2, 1, 4, 5, 3
(C) 1, 4, 2, 5, 3
(D) 2, 4, 5, 1, 3

Answer: A
Explanation:
Over Population (2) leads to Unemployment (4). 
Unemployment causes Poverty (1). 
Poverty leads to malnutrition and Disease (5), which ultimately results in Death (3).
Correct sequence: 2, 4, 1, 5, 3.

Q6. 1. Yarn  2. Plant  3. Saree  4. Cotton  5. Cloth
(A) 2, 4, 1, 5, 3
(B) 2, 4, 5, 1, 3
(C) 2, 4, 3, 5, 1
(D) 2, 1, 4, 5, 3

Answer: A
Explanation:
From a Cotton Plant (2), we get raw Cotton (4).

The Cloth is tailored/designed into a Saree (3).

(B) 4, 2, 3, 5, 1
(C) 4, 2, 5, 1, 3
(D) 2, 4, 5, 3, 1

Answer: A
Explanation:
Arranging the numbers in ascending order of magnitude:
Hundred (10^2) (4)
Thousand (10^3) (2)
Million (10^6) (5)
Billion (10^9) (3)
Trillion (10^12) (1)
Correct sequence: 4, 2, 5, 3, 1.
