Directions (Q1–Q5): Read the following information carefully and answer the questions.

Eight persons sit in two parallel rows containing four persons each, in such a way that there is an equal distance between adjacent persons.
In Row 1: A, B, C, and D sit facing South.
In Row 2: P, Q, R, and S sit facing North.
Therefore, in the given seating arrangement, each member seated in a row faces another member of the other row.

• A sits at the extreme right end of the row.
• A faces P.
• R sits second to the right of P.
• The person facing R sits to the immediate left of D.
• B sits to the left of C.
• Q does not face B.

Q1. Who faces S?
(A) A
(B) B
(C) C
(D) D

Answer: B
Explanation:
Step 1: Row 1 faces South (Left means viewer's right, Right means viewer's left). Row 2 faces North. Positions are 1 to 4 from viewer's left to right.
Step 2: A sits at the extreme right end of Row 1 → Pos 1 (viewer's left).
Step 3: A faces P → P is at Pos 1 in Row 2.
Step 4: R is 2nd right of P (North facing) → R is at Pos 3.
Step 5: The person facing R is at Pos 3. This person sits immediate left of D. For South facing, immediate left is towards viewer's right. So D must be at Pos 2 (left of D is Pos 3).
Step 6: B sits to the left of C (Row 1). For South facing, left is viewer's right. Empty spots: 3, 4. So C=3, B=4. (Row 1: A, D, C, B).
Step 7: Q does not face B(4). Empty in Row 2: 2, 4. So Q=2, S=4. (Row 2: P, Q, R, S).
Final Arrangement (L to R):
Row 1 (South): A, D, C, B
Row 2 (North): P, Q, R, S
S is at Pos 4, facing B.
Answer: B → (B)

----------------------------------------

Q2. What is the position of Q with respect to R?
(A) Immediate left
(B) Immediate right
(C) Second to the left
(D) Second to the right

Answer: A
Explanation:
Row 2: P, Q, R, S (facing North).
Q is at Pos 2, R is at Pos 3.
Q is to the immediate left of R.
Answer: Immediate left → (A)

Q3. Which of the following pairs sit at the extreme ends of their respective rows?
(A) A, B and P, R
(B) A, C and Q, S
(C) D, B and P, S
(D) A, B and P, S

Answer: D
Explanation:
Row 1 ends: A (Pos 1) and B (Pos 4).
Row 2 ends: P (Pos 1) and S (Pos 4).
Answer: A, B and P, S → (D)

Q4. How many persons sit between A and C?
(A) None
(B) One
(C) Two
(D) Three

Explanation:
Row 1: A(1), D(2), C(3), B(4).

Q5. Who sits to the immediate right of the person who faces D?
(A) P
(B) Q
(C) R
(D) S

Explanation:

Q faces North, so the person to the immediate right of Q is R (Pos 3).

Directions (Q6–Q10): Read the following information carefully and answer the questions.

Ten persons sit in two parallel rows containing five persons each.
In Row 1: U, V, W, X, and Y sit facing South.
In Row 2: F, G, H, I, and J sit facing North.
Each person in Row 1 faces a person in Row 2.

• W sits third to the right of Y, who sits at an extreme end of the row.
• The person facing W sits second to the left of I.
• Only one person sits between I and F.
• V faces the person who sits to the immediate right of F.
• X sits to the right of U.
• H sits to the immediate left of the person who faces U.
• G does not face Y.

Refer to diagram: SVG/Q3.svg

Q6. Who faces X?
(A) G
(B) F
(C) H
(D) J

Answer: A
Explanation:
Step 1: Row 1 faces South. Y is at an extreme end. If Y=Pos 1(Left), right is off the grid. So Y must be at Pos 5(Right). 3rd right of Y is Pos 2 → W=2.
Step 2: Person facing W is at Pos 2. This person is 2nd left of I (North facing) → I=4.
Step 3: One person between I(4) and F → F=2.
Step 4: Immediate right of F(2) is Pos 3. V faces Pos 3 → V=3.
Step 5: X is right of U (South facing: right is viewer's left). Empty spots in Row 1: 1, 4. So X=1, U=4.
Step 6: U(4) faces Pos 4 (which is I). H is immediate left of I → H=3.
Step 7: G does not face Y(5). Empty in Row 2: 1, 5. So G=1, J=5.
Final Arrangement (L to R):
Row 1 (South): X, W, V, U, Y
Row 2 (North): G, F, H, I, J
X is at Pos 1 and faces G.
Answer: G → (A)

----------------------------------------

Q7. What is the position of V with respect to Y?
(A) Second to the left
(B) Third to the right
(C) Immediate right
(D) Second to the right

Answer: D
Explanation:
Row 1: X(1), W(2), V(3), U(4), Y(5). They face South.
Left/Right is inverted for the viewer. Y is at 5. The right of Y is 4, 3, 2, 1.
V is at 3, which is 2 positions to the right of Y.
Answer: Second to the right → (D)

Explanation:
X(1), Y(5), and G(1) sit at the extreme ends of their respective rows.
H(3) sits exactly in the middle of Row 2.

Directions (Q11–Q15): Read the following information carefully and answer the questions.

Fourteen persons sit in two parallel rows containing seven persons each.
In Row 1: A, B, C, D, E, F, and G sit facing South.
In Row 2: P, Q, R, S, T, U, and V sit facing North.

• P sits third to the right of S, and neither of them sits at an extreme end.
• The person facing P sits second to the left of B.
• Only two persons sit between B and A. A does not sit at an extreme end.
• C sits to the immediate right of A.
• The person facing C sits to the immediate left of V.
• U sits second to the left of T.
• Q does not sit at an extreme end.
• The person facing T sits to the immediate right of D.
• E sits to the left of F but to the right of G.

Refer to diagram: SVG/Q11.svg

Q11. Who faces F?
(A) Q
(B) V
(C) R
(D) U

Answer: C
Explanation:
Step 1: Row 2 faces North. S and P are not at ends. P is 3rd right of S. Possibilities: S=2,P=5 or S=3,P=6.
If S=3, P=6: Facing P is Pos 6. 2nd left of B (South facing, left is viewer's right) is 6 → B=4. Two between B(4) and A → A=1 or 7. But A is not at an end. So S=3 is invalid.
Step 2: S=2, P=5. Facing P is Pos 5. 2nd left of B is 5 → B=3.
Step 3: Two between B(3) and A → A=6. (Valid, not an end).
Step 4: C is immediate right of A(6). For South, right is viewer's left → C=5.
Step 5: Person facing C(5) is P(5). P sits immediate left of V → V=6.
Step 6: U 2nd left of T. Empty in Row 2: 1, 3, 4, 7. T cannot be 4 (U=2 taken by S), T cannot be 7 (U=5 taken by P). So T=3, U=1.
Step 7: Q is not at an end → Q=4. Remaining for Row 2 is R(7). Row 2: U, S, T, Q, P, V, R.
Step 8: Person facing T(3) is B(3). B sits immediate right of D. For South, right is viewer's left → D=4.
Step 9: Empty in Row 1: 1, 2, 7. E is left of F (viewer's right) and right of G (viewer's left). Viewer order: G, E, F → G=1, E=2, F=7.
Final Row 1: G, E, B, D, C, A, F.
F is at Pos 7, facing R(7).
Answer: R → (C)

----------------------------------------

Q12. What is the position of D with respect to E?
(A) Immediate right
(B) Immediate left
(C) Second to the left
(D) Second to the right

Answer: C
Explanation:
Row 1: G(1), E(2), B(3), D(4), C(5), A(6), F(7). (Facing South).
D is at Pos 4, E is at Pos 2.
For South facing, Left hand points to viewer's right. So Pos 4 is to the left of Pos 2.
Since it is two spots away, D is second to the left of E.
Answer: Second to the left → (C)

Q13. How many persons sit between U and the person who faces A?
(A) Two
(B) Three
(C) Four
(D) Five

Answer: C
Explanation:
U is at Pos 1 (Row 2). A is at Pos 6 (Row 1), so A faces V(6) in Row 2.
Persons sitting between U(1) and V(6) are S(2), T(3), Q(4), and P(5). Total = 4 persons.
Answer: Four → (C)

Q15. Which of the following statements is true regarding Q?
(A) Q faces D
(B) Q sits adjacent to U
(C) Q sits second to the right of V
(D) Exactly one person sits between Q and R

Answer: A
Explanation:
Arrangement checks:
(A) Q is at Pos 4 in Row 2, D is at Pos 4 in Row 1. Q faces D (True).
(B) Q(4) is adjacent to T(3) and P(5), not U(1) (False).
(C) Q(4) is second to the left of V(6), not right (False).
(D) Q(4) and R(7) have two persons between them (P, V) (False).
Answer: Q faces D → (A)
