Directions (Q1–Q5): Answer the questions based on the basic linear arrangement formulas.

Q1. In a row of 40 students facing North, Amit is 15th from the left end. What is his rank from the right end?
(A) 24th
(B) 25th
(C) 26th
(D) 27th

Answer: C
Explanation:
Rank from Right = Total - Rank from Left + 1
Rank = 40 - 15 + 1 = 26th.

----------------------------------------

Q2. Rahul is 12th from the left end and 20th from the right end in a row of boys. How many boys are there in the row?
(A) 30
(B) 31
(C) 32
(D) 33

Answer: B
Explanation:
Total = Left + Right - 1
Total = 12 + 20 - 1 = 31.

----------------------------------------

Q3. In a queue, Priya is 8th from the front and Riya is 12th from the back. If there are 5 people standing strictly between them, what is the total number of people in the queue? (Assume non-overlapping).
(A) 23
(B) 24
(C) 25
(D) 26

Answer: C
Explanation:
In a non-overlapping scenario: Total = Front + Back + Between
Total = 8 + 12 + 5 = 25.

----------------------------------------

Q4. In a row of 50 students, Esha is 20th from the left and Farah is 15th from the right. How many students are sitting between them?
(A) 14
(B) 15
(C) 16
(D) 17

Answer: B
Explanation:
Sum of ranks = 20 + 15 = 35. Since 35 < 50, it is a non-overlapping case.
Students between = Total - Sum of Ranks = 50 - 35 = 15.

----------------------------------------

Q5. Gautam is exactly in the middle of a row containing 31 students. What is his rank from the left end?
(A) 15th
(B) 16th
(C) 17th
(D) 18th

Answer: B
Explanation:
For an odd number of students (N), the exact middle position is (N + 1) / 2.
Rank = (31 + 1) / 2 = 16th.

Directions (Q6–Q10): Study the interchanging and shifting scenarios carefully.

Q6. A is 12th from the left and B is 18th from the right. When they interchange their positions, A becomes 25th from the left. What is the total number of students in the row?
(A) 40
(B) 41
(C) 42
(D) 43

Answer: C
Explanation:
A's new position is the same physical seat as B's old position. 
So, this seat is 25th from the left and 18th from the right.
Total = Left + Right - 1 = 25 + 18 - 1 = 42.

----------------------------------------

Q7. In the previous question (Q6), what will be B's new position from the right end after the interchange?
(A) 30th
(B) 31st
(C) 32nd
(D) 33rd

Answer: B
Explanation:
B's new seat is A's old seat, which is 12th from the left. 
We know Total = 42.
B's Rank from Right = Total - Left Rank + 1 = 42 - 12 + 1 = 31st.
Alternatively, shift difference: A shifted 25 - 12 = +13 places. B also shifts +13 places: 18 + 13 = 31st.

----------------------------------------

Q8. P is 15th from the left end. If he shifts 5 places to his right, he becomes exactly in the middle of the row. What is the total number of people in the row?
(A) 38
(B) 39
(C) 40
(D) 41

Answer: B
Explanation:
New position of P from left = 15 + 5 = 20th.
Since he is exactly in the middle, there are 19 people to his left and 19 to his right.
Total = 19 + 1 + 19 = 39.

----------------------------------------

Q9. Q is 14th from the right end. If he shifts 4 places to his left, he becomes 18th from the left end. What is the total number of students?
(A) 34
(B) 35
(C) 36
(D) 37

Answer: B
Explanation:
Shifting 4 places to the left from the right end means his rank from the right increases.
New Rank from right = 14 + 4 = 18th.
His rank from left is given as 18th.
Total = 18 + 18 - 1 = 35.

----------------------------------------

Q10. In a row of 60 students, R is 25th from the left and S is 30th from the right. T sits exactly between them. What is T's position from the left end?
(A) 27th
(B) 28th
(C) 29th
(D) 30th

Answer: B
Explanation:
S is 30th from right -> (60 - 30 + 1) = 31st from left.
R is 25th from left. 
T is exactly midway between the 25th and 31st positions.
T's rank = (25 + 31) / 2 = 56 / 2 = 28th from the left.

Directions (Q11–Q15): Study the overlapping and complex ranking scenarios carefully.

Q11. X is 25th from the left and Y is 30th from the right. If there are 10 students between them, what is the minimum possible total number of students in the row?
(A) 41
(B) 43
(C) 45
(D) 55

Answer: B
Explanation:
To find the minimum, we must assume an overlapping scenario.
Formula for overlap: Total = (Sum of Ranks) - (Between) - 2
Total = (25 + 30) - 10 - 2 = 55 - 12 = 43.

----------------------------------------

Q12. In a row, M is 15th from the left and N is 22nd from the right. O is exactly between them. If O is 26th from the left, find the total number of students (assuming non-overlapping).
(A) 55
(B) 58
(C) 60
(D) 62

Answer: B
Explanation:
M is 15th from left. O is 26th from left.
Number of students strictly between M and O = 26 - 15 - 1 = 10.
Since O is exactly midway, there are also 10 students strictly between O and N.
Total students between M and N = 10 (left of O) + 1 (O) + 10 (right of O) = 21.
Total = Left(M) + Right(N) + Between = 15 + 22 + 21 = 58.

Q13. In a row of 50 students, A is 35th from the left and B is 40th from the right. How many students are sitting between A and B?
(A) 20
(B) 21
(C) 23
(D) 25

Answer: C
Explanation:
Sum of ranks = 35 + 40 = 75. 
Since 75 > 50, they overlap.
In overlap: Between = Sum of ranks - Total - 2
Between = 75 - 50 - 2 = 23 students.

----------------------------------------

Q14. In a row of 80 students, the ratio of girls to boys is 3:5. Ram, a boy, is 25th from the left end. If there are 10 girls to the left of Ram, how many boys are to his right?
(A) 33
(B) 34
(C) 35
(D) 36

Answer: C
Explanation:
Total = 80. Girls = (3/8)*80 = 30. Boys = (5/8)*80 = 50.
Ram is 25th, so 24 students are to his left.
Out of 24, 10 are girls, meaning 14 are boys.
Ram himself is a boy (15th boy from left).
Remaining boys to his right = Total boys (50) - 14 (left) - 1 (Ram) = 35.

----------------------------------------

Q15. In a queue of 65 people, A is 18th from the front and B is 25th from the back. C is strictly in the middle of the queue. How many people are standing strictly between B and C?
(A) 7
(B) 8
(C) 9
(D) 10

Answer: A
Explanation:
C is in the middle of 65 -> Rank = (65 + 1) / 2 = 33rd from the front.
B is 25th from the back -> Rank = 65 - 25 + 1 = 41st from the front.
Number of people between C (33) and B (41) = 41 - 33 - 1 = 7.
