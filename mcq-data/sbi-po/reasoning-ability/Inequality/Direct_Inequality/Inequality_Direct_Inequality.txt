Directions (Q1–Q5): In these questions, relationship between different elements is shown in the statements. The statements are followed by conclusions. Study the conclusions based on the given statements and select the appropriate answer.

Q1. 
Statements: P ≥ Q = R > S > T
Conclusions:
I. P > S
II. Q > T
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Both conclusion I and II follow
(D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

Answer: C
Explanation:
From the statements, P ≥ Q = R > S. Comparing P and S, we get P > S. Thus, Conclusion I follows.
Comparing Q and T, we get Q = R > S > T, which leads to Q > T. Thus, Conclusion II follows.
Answer: Both conclusion I and II follow → (C)

----------------------------------------

Q2. 
Statements: A < B ≤ C = D, E ≥ D
Conclusions:
I. A < E
II. C ≤ E
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Both conclusion I and II follow
(D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

Answer: C
Explanation:
Combining the statements: A < B ≤ C = D ≤ E.
For Conclusion I: A < B ≤ C = D ≤ E → A < E. Thus, Conclusion I follows.
For Conclusion II: C = D ≤ E → C ≤ E. Thus, Conclusion II follows.
Answer: Both conclusion I and II follow → (C)

Q3. 
Statements: M ≥ N = O < P, Q > P
Conclusions:
I. M > O
II. N < Q
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Both conclusion I and II follow
(D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

Answer: B
Explanation:
Combining the statements: M ≥ N = O < P < Q.
For Conclusion I: M ≥ N = O → M ≥ O. The relationship is 'greater than or equal to', not strictly 'greater than'. Thus, Conclusion I does not follow.
For Conclusion II: N = O < P < Q → N < Q. Thus, Conclusion II follows.
Answer: Only conclusion II follows → (B)

----------------------------------------

Q4. 
Statements: W = X ≥ Y > Z, Y ≤ A < B
Conclusions:
I. W > Z
II. X ≥ A
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Both conclusion I and II follow
(D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

Answer: A
Explanation:
For Conclusion I: W = X ≥ Y > Z → W > Z. Thus, Conclusion I follows.
For Conclusion II: Comparing X and A via Y: X ≥ Y ≤ A. Because the signs face opposite directions (≥ and ≤), no definite relation can be established between X and A. Thus, Conclusion II does not follow.
Answer: Only conclusion I follows → (A)

Q5. 
Statements: G > H ≤ I = J < K
Conclusions:
I. G > I
II. H < K
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Both conclusion I and II follow
(D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

Answer: B
Explanation:
For Conclusion I: G > H ≤ I. The opposite signs (> and ≤) between G and I mean no direct relation can be established. Thus, Conclusion I does not follow.
For Conclusion II: H ≤ I = J < K → H < K. Thus, Conclusion II follows.
Answer: Only conclusion II follows → (B)

Directions (Q6–Q10): In these questions, the statements are separated by commas. Combine the statements logically to evaluate the conclusions.

Q6. 
Statements: A > B ≥ C, C = D < E, E ≤ F
Conclusions:
I. A > D
II. B < F
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Either conclusion I or II follows
(D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

Answer: A
Explanation:
Combined chain for A and D: A > B ≥ C = D → A > D. Thus, Conclusion I follows.
Combined chain for B and F: B ≥ C = D < E ≤ F. Since the signs are opposite (≥ and <) between B and F, no relation can be established. Thus, Conclusion II does not follow.
Answer: Only conclusion I follows → (A)

Directions (Q11–Q15): Study the complex multi-statement inequalities to evaluate the given conclusions, paying special attention to Either-Or cases.

Q11. 
Statements: M = N ≥ O < P, Q ≤ O = R, S > R
Conclusions:
I. M ≥ Q
II. P > S
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Either conclusion I or II follows
(D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

Answer: A
Explanation:
Combined chain for M and Q: M = N ≥ O ≥ Q → M ≥ Q. Thus, Conclusion I follows.
Combined chain for P and S: P > O = R < S. The opposite signs (> and <) block any direct relation between P and S. Thus, Conclusion II does not follow.
Answer: Only conclusion I follows → (A)

----------------------------------------

Q12. 
Statements: K ≥ L = M > N, O ≤ M = P
Conclusions:
I. K > O
II. K = O
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Either conclusion I or II follows
(D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

Answer: C
Explanation:
Combined chain for K and O: K ≥ L = M = P ≥ O → K ≥ O.
Since the definite relation between K and O is K ≥ O, it means K is either strictly greater than O OR exactly equal to O.
Since both possibilities are presented independently in Conclusions I and II, this forms a perfect "Either-Or" condition.
Answer: Either conclusion I or II follows → (C)

Q13. 
Statements: D ≤ E < F = G, H > G ≥ I, J = F
Conclusions:
I. D > H
II. J < I
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Either conclusion I or II follows
(D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

Answer: D
Explanation:
Combined chain for D and H: D ≤ E < F = G < H → D < H. Conclusion I states D > H, which is definitely false.
Combined chain for J and I: J = F = G ≥ I → J ≥ I. Conclusion II states J < I, which is false.
Since both conclusions are independently false, neither follows.
Answer: Neither conclusion I nor II follows → (D)

Q14. 
Statements: P < Q = R ≥ S, T > R ≤ U, V = T
Conclusions:
I. V > S
II. U ≥ Q
III. P < U
(A) Only I and II follow
(B) Only II and III follow
(C) All I, II, and III follow
(D) Only I and III follow

Answer: C
Explanation:
Combined chain for V and S: V = T > R ≥ S → V > S. (Conclusion I is True).
Combined chain for U and Q: U ≥ R = Q → U ≥ Q. (Conclusion II is True).
Combined chain for P and U: P < Q = R ≤ U → P < U. (Conclusion III is True).
Answer: All I, II, and III follow → (C)

Q15. 
Statements: A ≤ B < C = D, E ≥ D < F, G = E > H
Conclusions:
I. G > C
II. F > B
III. A < E
(A) Only I and II follow
(B) Only II and III follow
(C) Only I and III follow
(D) All I, II, and III follow

Answer: B
Explanation:
Combined chain for G and C: G = E ≥ D = C → G ≥ C. Conclusion I states G > C, which lacks the '=' possibility, so it is false.
Combined chain for F and B: F > D = C > B → F > B. (Conclusion II is True).
Combined chain for A and E: A ≤ B < C = D ≤ E → A < E. (Conclusion III is True).
Answer: Only II and III follow → (B)
