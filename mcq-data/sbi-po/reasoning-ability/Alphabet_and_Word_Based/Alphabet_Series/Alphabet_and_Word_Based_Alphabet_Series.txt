Directions (Q1–Q5): Find the missing term in the given alphabet series.

Q1. A, C, F, J, O, ?
(A) U
(B) T
(C) V
(D) S

Answer: A
Explanation:
The pattern is based on the forward gap between the letters:
A (+2) = C
C (+3) = F
F (+4) = J
J (+5) = O
O (+6) = U.

Q2. AZ, CX, EV, GT, ?
(A) IR
(B) HS
(C) KP
(D) JQ

Answer: A
Explanation:
First letter: A (+2) = C (+2) = E (+2) = G (+2) = I.
Second letter is the reverse alphabetical pair of the first letter (A-Z, C-X, E-V, G-T). The reverse pair of I is R.
Result: IR.

----------------------------------------

Q3. BDF, HJL, NPR, ?
(A) TVX
(B) UWY
(C) SUW
(D) TVY

Answer: A
Explanation:
Within each group, letters skip by +2: B(+2)D(+2)F.
Between groups, the gap from the last letter of one to the first of the next is +2: F(+2) = H.
Next group starts with R(+2) = T.
Group: T (+2) = V (+2) = X. Result: TVX.

----------------------------------------

Q4. W, U, R, N, I, ?
(A) C
(B) D
(C) E
(D) F

Answer: A
Explanation:
The backward positional gap increases by 1 each time:
W (-2) = U
U (-3) = R
R (-4) = N
N (-5) = I
I (-6) = C.

Q5. AB, DEF, HIJK, ?, STUVWX
(A) MNOPQ
(B) LMNOP
(C) LMNO
(D) MNOP

Answer: A
Explanation:
The number of letters in each group increases: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.
Gap between the last letter of a group and the first letter of the next is +2.
B (+2) = D. F (+2) = H. K (+2) = M.
The next group must start with M and have 5 continuous letters: M, N, O, P, Q.

Q12. In a specific alphabet mapping, a letter shifts forward by its own vowel/consonant status. Consonants shift +2, Vowels shift +3. What will the series 'B, E, K, O' become?
(A) D, H, M, R
(B) D, H, M, S
(C) E, G, M, R
(D) D, I, M, R

Answer: A
Explanation:
B (Consonant) -> +2 = D
E (Vowel) -> +3 = H
K (Consonant) -> +2 = M
O (Vowel) -> +3 = R
The resulting series is D, H, M, R.

Q13. Which of the following does NOT belong to the group?
(A) CFI
(B) UXA
(C) SVY
(D) DHL

Answer: C
Explanation:
Check the gaps:
C (+3) F (+3) I
U (+3) X (+3) A
D (+4) H (+4) L -> Wait, gap is +4.
Let's re-evaluate:
(A) C (+3) F (+3) I
(B) U (+3) X (+3) A
(C) S (+3) V (+3) Y
(D) D (+4) H (+4) L
D has +4 skips, while others have +3 skips. Thus, DHL is the odd one out.

Q15. If every alternate letter of the English alphabet starting from A is dropped (A, C, E... dropped), which letter will be the 5th to the right of the 4th letter from the left end of the new series?
(A) P
(B) R
(C) T
(D) V

Answer: B
Explanation:
New series (only even positional letters remain): B, D, F, H, J, L, N, P, R, T, V, X, Z.
4th letter from the left end = H.
5th to the right of H (4th) = 9th letter from the left end.
The 9th letter in this series is R. (2 * 9 = 18th letter in original alphabet).
