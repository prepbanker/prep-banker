Directions (Q1–Q5): Study the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
- Point A is 9m West of Point B.
- Point C is 12m South of Point B.
- Point D is 16m East of Point C.
- Point E is 20m North of Point D.
- Point F is 16m West of Point E.
- Point G is 9m West of Point F.

Refer to diagram: SVG/Q2.svg for a visual representation.

Q1. What is the shortest distance between Point A and Point G?
(A) 5m
(B) 8m
(C) 10m
(D) 12m

Answer: B
Explanation:
Let's assign coordinates (x, y) starting with B at (0,0).
A = (-9, 0)
C = (0, -12)
D = (16, -12)
E = (16, 8)  [since 20m North of D: -12 + 20 = 8]
F = (0, 8)   [16m West of E: 16 - 16 = 0]
G = (-9, 8)  [9m West of F: 0 - 9 = -9]
Point A is at (-9, 0) and Point G is at (-9, 8). The horizontal coordinates are the same, so the shortest distance is the vertical difference: 8 - 0 = 8m.

----------------------------------------

Q2. Point E is in which direction with respect to Point B?
(A) North-East
(B) North-West
(C) South-East
(D) South-West

Answer: A
Explanation:
B is at (0,0) and E is at (16, 8). Since both the x and y coordinates of E are positive relative to B, E is in the North-East direction with respect to B.

----------------------------------------

Q3. What is the shortest distance between Point C and Point F?
(A) 16m
(B) 18m
(C) 20m
(D) 24m

Answer: C
Explanation:
C is at (0, -12) and F is at (0, 8). Both lie on the same vertical line (y-axis). The total vertical distance is 8 - (-12) = 20m.

----------------------------------------

Q4. Point D is in which direction with respect to Point A?
(A) East
(B) South-East
(C) South-West
(D) North-East

Answer: B
Explanation:
A is at (-9, 0) and D is at (16, -12). Point D is to the right (East) and below (South) Point A. Therefore, D is South-East of A.

----------------------------------------

Q5. If Point H is 5m East of Point C, then what is the shortest distance between Point B and Point H?
(A) 12m
(B) 13m
(C) 15m
(D) 17m

Answer: B
Explanation:
C is at (0, -12). Point H is 5m East of C, so H is at (5, -12). 
B is at (0,0).
Using the Pythagorean theorem: Distance = √((5 - 0)² + (-12 - 0)²) = √(25 + 144) = √169 = 13m.

Directions (Q6–Q10): Study the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
Rahul starts from Point P, walks 10m towards the North to reach Point Q. Then he takes a right turn and walks 15m to reach Point R. Then he takes a right turn, walks 14m to reach Point S, and stops.
Simultaneously, Amit starts from Point M, which is 24m East of Point P. Amit walks 17m towards the North to reach Point N. He takes a left turn and walks 9m to reach Point O. Then he takes a left turn, walks 21m to reach Point T, and stops.

Refer to diagram: SVG/Q8.svg for a visual representation.

Q6. What is the shortest distance between Point Q and Point N?
(A) 24m
(B) 25m
(C) 26m
(D) 28m

Answer: B
Explanation:
Let P be (0,0). 
Rahul's path: Q=(0, 10). R=(15, 10). S=(15, -4).
Amit's path: M=(24, 0). N=(24, 17). O=(15, 17). T=(15, -4).
Point Q is (0, 10) and Point N is (24, 17). 
Shortest distance = √((24 - 0)² + (17 - 10)²) = √(24² + 7²) = √(576 + 49) = √625 = 25m.

Q7. In which direction is Point M with respect to Point R?
(A) South-East
(B) South-West
(C) North-East
(D) North-West

Answer: A
Explanation:
Point M is at (24, 0) and Point R is at (15, 10). M is to the right (East) and below (South) R. Thus, M is South-East of R.

Q8. What is the shortest distance between Rahul's final position and Amit's final position?
(A) 5m
(B) 10m
(C) 15m
(D) 0m

Answer: D
Explanation:
Rahul's final position is S (15, -4). Amit's final position is T (15, -4). Since both their coordinates are exactly the same, they meet at the same point. The distance is 0m.

Q9. In which direction is Point O with respect to Point P?
(A) North-West
(B) North-East
(C) South-East
(D) South-West

Answer: B
Explanation:
P is at (0,0) and O is at (15, 17). Both x and y coordinates are positive, meaning O is in the North-East direction with respect to P.

Q10. If Point Y is 10m South of Point R, what is the distance between Point P and Point Y?
(A) 10m
(B) 12m
(C) 15m
(D) 20m

Answer: C
Explanation:
R is at (15, 10). Point Y is 10m South of R, which places Y at (15, 0). Point P is at (0,0). Since both lie on the x-axis, the distance is simply 15 - 0 = 15m.

Directions (Q11–Q15): Study the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
'A @(X) B' means 'A is X m East of B'
'A #(X) B' means 'A is X m West of B'
'A $(X) B' means 'A is X m North of B'
'A %(X) B' means 'A is X m South of B'

For Q11 to Q13, consider the following expression:
P @(15) Q $(12) R #(20) S %(18) T @(5) U

Refer to diagram: SVG/Q11.svg for a visual representation.

Q11. What is the shortest distance between Point P and Point U?
(A) 5m
(B) 6m
(C) 8m
(D) 10m

Answer: B
Explanation:
Decoding the expression:
P @(15) Q → P is 15m East of Q. Let Q=(0,0), so P=(15,0).
Q $(12) R → Q is 12m North of R. R=(0,-12).
R #(20) S → R is 20m West of S. S=(20,-12).
S %(18) T → S is 18m South of T. T=(20,6).
T @(5) U → T is 5m East of U. U=(15,6).
P is at (15,0) and U is at (15,6). Both lie on the same vertical line. Shortest distance = 6 - 0 = 6m.

----------------------------------------

Q12. In which direction is Point Q with respect to Point S?
(A) North-East
(B) North-West
(C) South-East
(D) South-West

Answer: B
Explanation:
Q is at (0,0) and S is at (20, -12). Q is to the left (West) and above (North) S. Therefore, Q is North-West of S.

----------------------------------------

Q13. If Point W is 12m North of Point S, then what is the shortest distance between Point P and Point W?
(A) 4m
(B) 5m
(C) 6m
(D) 8m

Answer: B
Explanation:
S is at (20, -12). Point W is 12m North of S, so W=(20, 0). 
P is at (15, 0). Since both lie on the same horizontal line, the distance is 20 - 15 = 5m.

Directions (Q14–Q15): For the following questions, consider the new expression:
M #(8) N %(15) O @(17) P $(15) Q

Q14. In the new expression, what is the shortest distance between Point M and Point Q?
(A) 7m
(B) 8m
(C) 9m
(D) 10m

Answer: C
Explanation:
Decoding the new expression:
P $(15) Q → P is 15m North of Q. Let Q=(0,0), so P=(0,15).
O @(17) P → O is 17m East of P. O=(17,15).
N %(15) O → N is 15m South of O. N=(17,0).
M #(8) N → M is 8m West of N. M=(9,0).
M is at (9,0) and Q is at (0,0). The shortest distance is 9m.

----------------------------------------

Q15. In the new expression, in which direction is Point P with respect to Point M?
(A) North-East
(B) North-West
(C) South-East
(D) South-West

Answer: B
Explanation:
P is at (0, 15) and M is at (9, 0). Point P is to the left (West) and above (North) M. Therefore, P is North-West of M.
