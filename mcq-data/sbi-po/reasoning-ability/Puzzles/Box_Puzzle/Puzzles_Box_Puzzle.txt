Directions (Q1–Q2): Read the following information carefully and answer the questions.

Five boxes — P, Q, R, S, and T — are stacked one above the other from Position 1 (bottom) to Position 5 (top). Only one box occupies each position.

• Box Q is at the top.
• Box T is at the bottom.
• Box P is immediately above Box T.
• Box S is immediately below Box Q.
• Box R is placed between Box P and Box S (not adjacent to either).

Q1. Which box is placed at Position 3?
(A) Box P
(B) Box R
(C) Box S
(D) Box T

Answer: B
Explanation:
Step 1: Box T is at the bottom → Position 1.
Step 2: Box Q is at the top → Position 5.
Step 3: Box P is immediately above Box T → P is at Position 2.
Step 4: Box S is immediately below Box Q → S is at Position 4.
Step 5: Box R is between P (Position 2) and S (Position 4) → R is at Position 3.
Final Arrangement (bottom to top): T(1) → P(2) → R(3) → S(4) → Q(5)
Answer: Box R → (B)

----------------------------------------

Q2. How many boxes are placed between Box T and Box Q?
(A) 2
(B) 3
(C) 1
(D) 4

Answer: B
Explanation:
Box T is at Position 1 and Box Q is at Position 5.
Boxes in between: P (Position 2), R (Position 3), S (Position 4) → 3 boxes.
Answer: 3 → (B)

Q15. Eight boxes are stacked from Position 1 (bottom) to Position 8 (top). The weights of the boxes are 10 kg, 15 kg, 20 kg, 25 kg, 30 kg, 35 kg, 40 kg, and 45 kg (each weight used exactly once).

• The 10 kg box is at Position 1.
• The 15 kg box is immediately above the 10 kg box.
• The 20 kg box is at Position 3.
• The 35 kg box is at Position 4.
• The 30 kg box is at Position 5.
• The 25 kg box is immediately above the 30 kg box.
• The 45 kg box is at Position 8.
• The 40 kg box is immediately below the 45 kg box.

If the boxes at Positions 3 to 6 are rearranged such that the heaviest box among them is placed at Position 3 and the lightest is placed at Position 6 (weights decreasing upward within this range), what will be the weight of the box at Position 4 after rearrangement?
(A) 35 kg
(B) 25 kg
(C) 30 kg
(D) 20 kg

Answer: C
Explanation:
Step 1: Derive original arrangement:
  Pos 1: 10 kg | Pos 2: 15 kg | Pos 3: 20 kg | Pos 4: 35 kg
  Pos 5: 30 kg | Pos 6: 25 kg | Pos 7: 40 kg | Pos 8: 45 kg
Step 2: Weights at Positions 3 to 6: 20 kg, 35 kg, 30 kg, 25 kg.
Step 3: Rearrange so heaviest is at Position 3, decreasing upward:
  Pos 3: 35 kg (heaviest) | Pos 4: 30 kg | Pos 5: 25 kg | Pos 6: 20 kg (lightest)
Step 4: Weight at Position 4 after rearrangement = 30 kg.
Answer: 30 kg → (C)
