Directions (Q1–Q2): Read the following information carefully and answer the questions.

Four persons — Aman, Brinda, Charu, and Dev — each have their birthday in a different month among January, March, June, and September.

• Dev's birthday is in January.
• Aman's birthday is in the last of the four months (chronologically).
• Brinda's birthday is in the month that comes immediately before Charu's birthday among the given months.

Q1. Whose birthday falls in June?
(A) Aman
(B) Brinda
(C) Charu
(D) Dev

Answer: C
Explanation:
Step 1: Dev → January (given directly).
Step 2: Aman → September (last among Jan, Mar, Jun, Sep).
Step 3: Remaining months for Brinda and Charu: March and June.
         Brinda's month comes immediately before Charu's → Brinda = March, Charu = June.
Final Arrangement: January – Dev | March – Brinda | June – Charu | September – Aman
Answer: Charu → (C)

----------------------------------------

Q2. In which month is Brinda's birthday?
(A) January
(B) March
(C) June
(D) September

Answer: B
Explanation:
From the arrangement derived in Q1:
March → Brinda | June → Charu
Answer: March → (B)

Directions (Q3–Q5): Read the following information carefully and answer the questions.

Five persons — P, Q, R, S, and T — each celebrate their birthday in a different month among February, April, July, October, and December.

• T's birthday is in December.
• R's birthday is in February.
• S's birthday is in October.
• P's birthday comes before Q's birthday (chronologically).
• P's birthday is in April and Q's birthday is in July.

Refer to diagram: SVG/Q3.svg

Q3. Whose birthday falls in July?
(A) P
(B) Q
(C) S
(D) T

Answer: B
Explanation:
Step 1: R → February | P → April | Q → July | S → October | T → December (all directly given or derived).
Step 2: P (April) comes before Q (July) ✓.
Final Arrangement: February – R | April – P | July – Q | October – S | December – T
Answer: Q → (B)

----------------------------------------

Q4. How many persons have their birthday before October?
(A) 2
(B) 3
(C) 4
(D) 1

Answer: B
Explanation:
Months before October: February (R), April (P), July (Q) → 3 persons.
Answer: 3 → (B)

Q5. Whose birthday falls between Q's and T's birthday (chronologically)?
(A) P
(B) R
(C) S
(D) None of the above

Answer: C
Explanation:
Q's birthday → July | T's birthday → December.
Among the five persons, October (S) falls between July and December.
Answer: S → (C)

Q10. Eight persons are scheduled to attend interviews in 8 different months (January to August) of the same year. Person X is scheduled exactly 3 months after Person Y. Person Y is scheduled in March. Person Z is scheduled immediately after Person X. In which month is Person Z scheduled?
(A) June
(B) July
(C) August
(D) May

Answer: B
Explanation:
Step 1: Y is scheduled in March.
Step 2: X is scheduled exactly 3 months after Y → March + 3 = June. So X is in June.
Step 3: Z is scheduled immediately after X (June) → Z is in July.
Answer: July → (B)

Q15. Eight persons — P, Q, R, S, T, U, V, and W — are each scheduled to attend a seminar in a different month from January to August of the same year.

• P is scheduled in January.
• U is scheduled in February.
• V is scheduled immediately after U.
• Q is scheduled exactly 3 months after P.
• S is scheduled immediately before T.
• T is scheduled in June.
• W is scheduled in July.
• R is scheduled in August.

The persons scheduled in the months of March, April, May, and June exchange their schedules in reverse order — i.e., the person originally in March moves to June, the person in April moves to May, the person in May moves to April, and the person in June moves to March.

Who will be scheduled in May after this rearrangement?
(A) S
(B) T
(C) Q
(D) V

Answer: C
Explanation:
Step 1: Derive original arrangement:
  P → January | U → February | V → March (immediately after U)
  Q → April (3 months after January) | S → May (immediately before T) | T → June | W → July | R → August
Step 2: Persons in March to June: V (March), Q (April), S (May), T (June).
Step 3: Reverse order within March–June:
  March → T | April → S | May → Q | June → V
Step 4: Person in May after rearrangement = Q.
Answer: Q → (C)
