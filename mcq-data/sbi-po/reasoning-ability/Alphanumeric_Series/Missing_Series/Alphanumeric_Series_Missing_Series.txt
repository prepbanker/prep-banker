Q1. P3C, R5F, T8I, V12L, ?
(A) X17O
(B) Y17O
(C) X16O
(D) X17P

Answer: A
Explanation:
First letter: P (+2) = R (+2) = T (+2) = V (+2) = X.
Number: 3 (+2) = 5 (+3) = 8 (+4) = 12 (+5) = 17.
Third letter: C (+3) = F (+3) = I (+3) = L (+3) = O.
Result: X17O.

Q10. Which term does NOT belong in the series?
1A2, 3C4, 7F8, 13J14, 21O22, 31T32
(A) 7F8
(B) 13J14
(C) 21O22
(D) 31T32

Answer: D
Explanation:
Let's check the patterns:
First number: 1 (+2) = 3 (+4) = 7 (+6) = 13 (+8) = 21 (+10) = 31. (Numbers are correct).
Second number is always First number + 1: 2, 4, 8, 14, 22, 32. (Correct).
Letter: A (+2) = C (+3) = F (+4) = J (+5) = O (+6) = U.
The given term is 31T32, but it should be 31U32.
Therefore, 31T32 is incorrect.

Q11. A1Z, E2V, I6R, M24N, ?
(A) Q120J
(B) Q100J
(C) Q120K
(D) P120J

Answer: A
Explanation:
First letter: A (+4) = E (+4) = I (+4) = M (+4) = Q.
Number: 1 (x2) = 2 (x3) = 6 (x4) = 24 (x5) = 120.
Second letter is the reverse pair of the first letter (A-Z, E-V, I-R, M-N, Q-J).
Result: Q120J.

Q12. What comes next in the sequence?
2, B, 4, D, 8, H, 16, P, 32, ?
(A) F
(B) G
(C) H
(D) J

Answer: A
Explanation:
The letters correspond to the alphabetical position of the preceding number, modulo 26 if necessary.
2 -> B (2nd)
4 -> D (4th)
8 -> H (8th)
16 -> P (16th)
32 -> 32nd letter = 32 - 26 = 6th letter = F.
Result: F.

Q14. In the series 4Z, 8X, 16V, 32T, ?, what is the 7th term?
(A) 256P
(B) 128R
(C) 256N
(D) 128N

Answer: C
Explanation:
Let's find the explicit formula.
Term 1: 4Z (2^2, 26)
Term 2: 8X (2^3, 24)
Term 3: 16V (2^4, 22)
Term 4: 32T (2^5, 20)
Term n: (2^(n+1)), Letter at (28 - 2n)
Term 7: Number = 2^(7+1) = 2^8 = 256.
Letter = 28 - 2(7) = 14th letter = N.
Result: 256N.
