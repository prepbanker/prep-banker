Topic: Fill in the Blanks
Subtopic: Single Blank

Q1. The sudden resignation of the CEO left the company in a state of complete ________.
(A) prosperity
(B) tranquility
(C) turmoil
(D) elation

Answer: C
Explanation: The sudden resignation of a top leader typically causes chaos or confusion. "Turmoil" means a state of great disturbance or uncertainty, which fits perfectly. 

Q2. Despite the heavy rain, the football match proceeded as ________.
(A) scheduled
(B) cancelled
(C) postponed
(D) delayed

Answer: A
Explanation: The word "Despite" introduces a contrast. Even though it was raining heavily, the match happened anyway. "Scheduled" correctly completes the phrase "proceeded as scheduled".

Q3. The teacher asked the students to ________ from talking during the final examination.
(A) engage
(B) refrain
(C) participate
(D) indulge

Answer: B
Explanation: The context is an examination where talking is not allowed. "Refrain" means to stop oneself from doing something, and it correctly takes the preposition "from".

Q4. Her exceptional performance in the interview ________ her a lucrative job offer.
(A) forfeited
(B) revoked
(C) secured
(D) diminished

Answer: C
Explanation: An exceptional performance would lead to gaining a job offer. "Secured" means succeeded in obtaining or achieving something.

Q5. The government has launched a new initiative to ________ poverty in rural areas.
(A) cultivate
(B) eradicate
(C) nourish
(D) amplify

Answer: B
Explanation: Poverty is a negative condition that a government would want to remove or destroy. "Eradicate" means to destroy completely or put an end to.

Q6. The author's latest novel is highly ________, drawing heavily from esoteric philosophical concepts.
(A) lucid
(B) superficial
(C) opaque
(D) engaging

Answer: C
Explanation: The sentence mentions the novel draws from "esoteric" (obscure, hard to understand) concepts. "Opaque" in this context means hard to understand or not transparent, aligning with "esoteric".

Q7. In an effort to ________ the growing public outrage, the mayor issued a public apology.
(A) incite
(B) mollify
(C) provoke
(D) instigate

Answer: B
Explanation: The mayor issued an apology to calm or reduce the public outrage. "Mollify" means to appease the anger or anxiety of someone.

Q8. The scientist's findings were met with skepticism, as they seemed to ________ established physical laws.
(A) corroborate
(B) substantiate
(C) contradict
(D) emulate

Answer: C
Explanation: Findings are met with skepticism if they go against what is already known. "Contradict" means to be in conflict with.

Q9. Her argument was so ________ that even her fiercest opponents were forced to agree with her.
(A) vacuous
(B) compelling
(C) frivolous
(D) redundant

Answer: B
Explanation: An argument that forces opponents to agree must be very strong and convincing. "Compelling" means evoking interest, attention, or admiration in a powerfully irresistible way.

Q10. The company’s financial records were intentionally ________ to hide the massive embezzlement.
(A) audited
(B) verified
(C) obfuscated
(D) streamlined

Answer: C
Explanation: To hide embezzlement, records must be made unclear or confusing. "Obfuscated" means rendered obscure, unclear, or unintelligible.

Q11. The diplomat’s ________ remarks during the press conference nearly triggered an international crisis.
(A) judicious
(B) incendiary
(C) pacifying
(D) innocuous

Answer: B
Explanation: Remarks that trigger an international crisis must be highly provocative. "Incendiary" means designed to cause fires, or in this context, tending to stir up conflict.

Q12. Despite his ________ demeanor in public, he was known to be quite ruthless in the boardroom.
(A) aggressive
(B) draconian
(C) affable
(D) imperious

Answer: C
Explanation: The word "Despite" indicates a contrast with "ruthless". We need a word meaning friendly or good-natured. "Affable" means friendly, good-natured, or easy to talk to.

Q13. The committee rejected the proposal, stating that the project was fundamentally ________ and bound to fail.
(A) viable
(B) pragmatic
(C) flawed
(D) infallible

Answer: C
Explanation: If the project is "bound to fail," it must have serious errors. "Flawed" means having a blemish or imperfection; fundamentally incorrect.

Q14. The ancient civilization was characterized by a deeply ________ society, where social mobility was virtually impossible.
(A) egalitarian
(B) stratified
(C) democratic
(D) fluid

Answer: B
Explanation: A society where social mobility is impossible is divided into rigid classes. "Stratified" means arranged in layers or classes.

15. His persistent refusal to accept the compromise was viewed as sheer ________ by the mediators.
(A) compliance
(B) obduracy
(C) flexibility
(D) prudence

Answer: B
Explanation: Persistent refusal to compromise is a sign of stubbornness. "Obduracy" means the quality of being stubbornly refusing to change one's opinion or course of action.
