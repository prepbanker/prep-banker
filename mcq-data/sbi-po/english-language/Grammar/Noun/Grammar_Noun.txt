Q1. Choose the grammatically correct sentence regarding noun usage:
(A) He gave me many advices before the interview.
(B) He gave me many pieces of advice before the interview.
(C) He gave me an advice before the interview.
(D) He gave me many advise before the interview.

Answer: B
Explanation: "Advice" is an uncountable noun. It cannot be pluralized as "advices" or used with "an". To make it countable, we use "pieces of advice".

Q2. Choose the correct noun to fill in the blank: The beautiful ________ of Kashmir attracts tourists from all over the world.
(A) sceneries
(B) scenery
(C) scenerys
(D) scenic

Answer: B
Explanation: "Scenery" is an uncountable noun and does not have a plural form ("sceneries").

Q3. Choose the grammatically correct sentence regarding noun usage:
(A) The furnitures in this room are completely ruined.
(B) The furniture in this room are completely ruined.
(C) The furniture in this room is completely ruined.
(D) A furniture in this room is completely ruined.

Answer: C
Explanation: "Furniture" is an uncountable collective noun. It takes a singular verb and cannot be pluralized as "furnitures".

Q4. Choose the correct noun phrase to fill in the blank: She has bought two ________ of bread from the bakery.
(A) loafs
(B) loaves
(C) loaf
(D) loave

Answer: B
Explanation: The plural form of the noun "loaf" is "loaves".

Q5. Choose the grammatically correct sentence regarding noun usage:
(A) His trousers is too tight for him.
(B) His trouser is too tight for him.
(C) His trousers are too tight for him.
(D) A trousers are too tight for him.

Answer: C
Explanation: Nouns that consist of two parts (like trousers, scissors, spectacles) are always plural and take a plural verb.

Q6. Choose the correct noun to fill in the blank: The committee has submitted ________ report after a detailed investigation.
(A) there
(B) its
(C) their
(D) his

Answer: B
Explanation: "Committee" is a collective noun. When it acts as a single, undivided unit (submitting a single report), it takes the singular pronoun "its".

Q7. Choose the correct noun phrase to fill in the blank: My mother-in-law's house is nearby, but my ________ houses are in the city.
(A) mothers-in-law
(B) mother-in-laws
(C) mothers-in-law's
(D) mother's-in-law

Answer: A
Explanation: Wait, "my mothers-in-law"? A person has only one mother-in-law. Let's fix the context.
Corrected Q7: The council invited several ________ to speak at the women's empowerment seminar.
(A) mother-in-laws
(B) mothers-in-law
(C) mothers-in-laws
(D) mother-in-law

Answer: B
Explanation: To make a compound noun plural, the 's' is added to the principal or main word. Therefore, "mother-in-law" becomes "mothers-in-law".

Q8. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: A large number of ________ were grazing in the field.
(A) sheeps
(B) sheep
(C) sheepes
(D) ship

Answer: B
Explanation: The noun "sheep" has the same form in both singular and plural. "Sheeps" is grammatically incorrect.

Q9. Choose the grammatically correct sentence regarding noun usage:
(A) Mathematics are a difficult subject for many students.
(B) Mathematics is a difficult subject for many students.
(C) Mathematic is a difficult subject for many students.
(D) Mathematics has a difficult subject for many students.

Answer: B
Explanation: Nouns ending in "-ics" like Mathematics, Physics, and Politics are singular when referring to a subject of study and take a singular verb.

Q10. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: I need to buy some new ________ for my laboratory.
(A) equipments
(B) equipment
(C) piece of equipments
(D) equip

Answer: B
Explanation: "Equipment" is an uncountable noun. It does not take an 's' to form a plural.

Q11. Choose the grammatically correct sentence regarding noun usage:
(A) Five dozens eggs were bought from the market.
(B) Five dozen egg were bought from the market.
(C) Five dozen eggs were bought from the market.
(D) Five dozens of eggs were bought from the market.

Answer: C
Explanation: When a definite numeral (like 'five') precedes nouns denoting measure, number, or weight (like dozen, score, million), the noun remains in the singular form ("dozen"). The object counted takes the plural ("eggs").

Q12. Choose the correct phrase to fill in the blank: He is a man of ________.
(A) words
(B) word
(C) wordings
(D) letters

Answer: D
Wait, "man of words" is not the common idiom. "Man of his word" is. Or "Man of letters".
Corrected Q12: Shakespeare was known to be a man of ________, celebrated for his vast literary contributions.
(A) words
(B) word
(C) letters
(D) letters'

Answer: C
Explanation: "A man of letters" is an idiom used to describe a male scholar or author. The noun "letters" here means literature or learning.

Q13. Choose the grammatically correct sentence regarding noun usage:
(A) The data is inconclusive regarding the new medical treatment.
(B) The datum are inconclusive regarding the new medical treatment.
(C) The datas are inconclusive regarding the new medical treatment.
(D) The data are inconclusive regarding the new medical treatment.

Answer: D
Explanation: In strict, formal grammar (often tested in banking exams), "data" is the plural of "datum". Therefore, it should correctly take the plural verb "are".

Q14. Choose the correct phrase to fill in the blank: The police caught the thief by his ________.
(A) hairs
(B) hair
(C) strand of hairs
(D) hair's

Answer: B
Explanation: "Hair" is typically treated as an uncountable mass noun when referring to the entire collection of hair on someone's head.

15. Choose the grammatically correct sentence regarding noun usage:
(A) It is a three-miles walk from my house to the university.
(B) It is a three-mile walk from my house to the university.
(C) It is three-mile walk from my house to the university.
(D) It is a three-miles walks from my house to the university.

Answer: B
Explanation: When a noun with a numeral acts as a compound adjective modifying another noun ("walk"), it must be in the singular form ("three-mile", not "three-miles").
