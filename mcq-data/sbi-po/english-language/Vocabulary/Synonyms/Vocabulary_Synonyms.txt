Subtopic: Synonyms

Q1. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **LUCID**
(A) Obscure
(B) Confusing
(C) Clear
(D) Vague

Answer: C
Explanation: "Lucid" means expressed clearly or easy to understand. "Clear" is the exact synonym.

Q2. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **ABUNDANT**
(A) Scarce
(B) Plentiful
(C) Minimal
(D) Depleted

Answer: B
Explanation: "Abundant" means existing or available in large quantities. "Plentiful" is the exact synonym.

Q3. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **CANDID**
(A) Deceitful
(B) Frank
(C) Guarded
(D) Secretive

Answer: B
Explanation: "Candid" means truthful and straightforward. "Frank" is the exact synonym.

Q4. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **MITIGATE**
(A) Aggravate
(B) Alleviate
(C) Intensify
(D) Provoke

Answer: B
Explanation: "Mitigate" means to make less severe, harmful, or painful. "Alleviate" is the exact synonym.

Q5. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **DILIGENT**
(A) Industrious
(B) Lethargic
(C) Careless
(D) Indifferent

Answer: A
Explanation: "Diligent" means having or showing care and conscientiousness in one's work or duties. "Industrious" (hard-working) is the exact synonym.

Q6. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **EPHEMERAL**
(A) Eternal
(B) Fleeting
(C) Permanent
(D) Enduring

Answer: B
Explanation: "Ephemeral" means lasting for a very short time. "Fleeting" is the exact synonym.

Q7. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **PRAGMATIC**
(A) Idealistic
(B) Practical
(C) Theoretical
(D) Romantic

Answer: B
Explanation: "Pragmatic" means dealing with things sensibly and realistically in a way that is based on practical rather than theoretical considerations. "Practical" is the exact synonym.

Q8. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **UBIQUITOUS**
(A) Rare
(B) Omnipresent
(C) Isolated
(D) Localized

Answer: B
Explanation: "Ubiquitous" means present, appearing, or found everywhere. "Omnipresent" is the exact synonym.

Q9. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **METICULOUS**
(A) Sloppy
(B) Fastidious
(C) Hasty
(D) Reckless

Answer: B
Explanation: "Meticulous" means showing great attention to detail; very careful and precise. "Fastidious" is the closest synonym among the choices.

Q10. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **VINDICTIVE**
(A) Forgiving
(B) Revengeful
(C) Merciful
(D) Compassionate

Answer: B
Explanation: "Vindictive" means having or showing a strong or unreasoning desire for revenge. "Revengeful" is the exact synonym.

Q11. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **ESOTERIC**
(A) Commonplace
(B) Abstruse
(C) Evident
(D) Familiar

Answer: B
Explanation: "Esoteric" means intended for or likely to be understood by only a small number of people with a specialized knowledge or interest. "Abstruse" (hard to understand) is the closest synonym.

Q12. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **OBSEQUIOUS**
(A) Domineering
(B) Sycophantic
(C) Assertive
(D) Arrogant

Answer: B
Explanation: "Obsequious" means obedient or attentive to an excessive or servile degree. "Sycophantic" (behaving or done in an obsequious way in order to gain advantage) is the exact synonym.

Q13. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **PERNICIOUS**
(A) Beneficial
(B) Innocuous
(C) Deleterious
(D) Salutary

Answer: C
Explanation: "Pernicious" means having a harmful effect, especially in a gradual or subtle way. "Deleterious" (causing harm or damage) is the exact synonym.

Q14. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **QUERULOUS**
(A) Contented
(B) Petulant
(C) Genial
(D) Complacent

Answer: B
Explanation: "Querulous" means complaining in a petulant or whining manner. "Petulant" (childishly sulky or bad-tempered) is the exact synonym.

15. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **TRUCULENT**
(A) Amiable
(B) Pugnacious
(C) Placid
(D) Serene

Answer: B
Explanation: "Truculent" means eager or quick to argue or fight; aggressively defiant. "Pugnacious" (eager or quick to argue, quarrel, or fight) is the exact synonym.
