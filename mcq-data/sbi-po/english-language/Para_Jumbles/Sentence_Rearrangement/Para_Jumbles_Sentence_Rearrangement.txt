Q1. Rearrange the following parts (A, B, C, D) to form a meaningful sentence:
A. to launch its new electric vehicle
B. next month in the international market
C. the automobile company is planning
D. amidst growing environmental concerns
(A) CABD
(B) CBAD
(C) ACBD
(D) CADB

Answer: D
Explanation: The subject is "the automobile company". C starts the sentence ("the automobile company is planning"). It is followed by the infinitive action A ("to launch its new electric vehicle"). D provides the context/reason ("amidst growing environmental concerns"). Finally, B provides the time and place ("next month in the international market"). The correct order is CADB. (Note: CABD is also grammatically possible, but CADB flows more naturally in standard business reporting). Let's adjust D and B to make CABD the absolute best.
Wait, let's look at CABD: "the automobile company is planning / to launch its new electric vehicle / next month in the international market / amidst growing environmental concerns." This is perfect.
Let's make (A) CABD the answer.
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: C introduces the subject and verb ("the automobile company is planning"). A follows with the action ("to launch its new electric vehicle"). B gives the time and place ("next month in the international market"). D concludes with the broader context ("amidst growing environmental concerns"). CABD is the most logical sequence.

Q2. Rearrange the following parts (A, B, C, D) to form a meaningful sentence:
A. had to be evacuated immediately
B. causing a massive structural collapse
C. because the foundation of the building gave way
D. all the residents of the apartment complex
(A) DABC
(B) CBAD
(C) DACB
(D) BCDA

Answer: C
Explanation: The main clause is "all the residents of the apartment complex (D) had to be evacuated immediately (A)". The subordinate clause explains why: "because the foundation of the building gave way (C) causing a massive structural collapse (B)". The correct order is DACB.

C. to achieve their long-term goals
D. but the result of sheer dedication for those
(A) BDAC
(B) BADC
(C) DABC
(D) ACBD

Answer: A
Explanation: B starts the independent clause ("success is not a matter of luck"). D follows with the contrast ("but the result of sheer dedication for those"). A modifies 'those' ("who are willing to work hard"). C completes the purpose ("to achieve their long-term goals"). The correct order is BDAC.
