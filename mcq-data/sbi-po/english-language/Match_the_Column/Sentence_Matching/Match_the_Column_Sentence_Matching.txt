Q1. Match the halves in Column I with those in Column II to form meaningful sentences.
Column I:
A. The company decided to launch its new product
B. Although he was feeling extremely exhausted
C. The government has introduced new subsidies

Column II:
D. he managed to complete the marathon in record time.
E. aimed at promoting renewable energy across the state.
F. ahead of the upcoming holiday season.

(A) A-F, B-D, C-E
(B) A-D, B-E, C-F
(C) A-E, B-F, C-D
(D) A-F, B-E, C-D

Answer: A
Explanation: 
A matches with F (launching product ahead of holiday season). 
B matches with D (exhausted, yet managed to complete the marathon). 
C matches with E (subsidies aimed at promoting renewable energy).
