Q1. Match the halves in Column I with those in Column II to form meaningful sentences.
Column I:
A. The bank has drastically lowered interest rates
B. Even though the presentation was hastily prepared
C. The municipal corporation initiated a massive drive

Column II:
D. it successfully conveyed the core message to the stakeholders.
E. to plant ten thousand saplings across the urban parks.
F. to stimulate borrowing and rejuvenate the stagnant economy.

(A) A-F, B-D, C-E
(B) A-D, B-E, C-F
(C) A-E, B-F, C-D
(D) A-F, B-E, C-D

Answer: A
Explanation: 
A matches with F (lowered interest rates -> to stimulate borrowing).
B matches with D (hastily prepared -> successfully conveyed message).
C matches with E (initiated a massive drive -> to plant saplings).
