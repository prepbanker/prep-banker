Q1. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: The aggressive marketing campaign was designed to ________ a sense of urgency among potential buyers.
(A) alleviate
(B) generate
(C) suppress
(D) eradicate

Answer: B
Explanation: The goal of an aggressive marketing campaign is to create or "generate" urgency. Alleviate, suppress, and eradicate mean to reduce or destroy.

Q2. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: The notoriously strict professor was completely ________ by the student's brilliant, unconventional theory.
(A) indifferent
(B) intrigued
(C) repelled
(D) apathetic

Answer: B
Explanation: A brilliant, unconventional theory would capture interest. "Intrigued" means to arouse curiosity or interest. Indifferent and apathetic mean uncaring.

Q3. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: The sudden drop in global oil prices will severely ________ the economic growth of nations heavily reliant on petroleum exports.
(A) accelerate
(B) stimulate
(C) impede
(D) facilitate

Answer: C
Explanation: If nations rely on exporting oil, a drop in prices will harm their economy. "Impede" means to delay or prevent someone or something by obstructing them.

Q4. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: Despite the CEO's desperate attempts to reassure the board, the company's financial situation remained dangerously ________.
(A) secure
(B) prosperous
(C) precarious
(D) robust

Answer: C
Explanation: If the CEO has to make desperate attempts to reassure the board, the situation is not good. "Precarious" means not securely held; dangerously likely to fall or collapse.

Q5. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: The defense attorney attempted to ________ the credibility of the key witness by exposing inconsistencies in his previous testimonies.
(A) bolster
(B) undermine
(C) elevate
(D) validate

Answer: B
Explanation: An attorney would try to damage the credibility of an opposing witness. "Undermine" means to lessen the effectiveness, power, or ability of something.

Q6. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: The ancient manuscript was so faded and fragmented that deciphering its true meaning proved to be a highly ________ task for the linguistic scholars.
(A) trivial
(B) effortless
(C) formidable
(D) mundane

Answer: C
Explanation: A faded, fragmented manuscript makes deciphering it very difficult. "Formidable" means inspiring fear or respect through being impressively large, powerful, intense, or capable (often used for difficult tasks).

Q7. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: The corrupt politician tried to ________ his illicit wealth by funneling it through a complex web of offshore shell companies.
(A) flaunt
(B) obfuscate
(C) declare
(D) squander

Answer: B
Explanation: A corrupt politician would want to hide illicit wealth. "Obfuscate" means to render obscure, unclear, or unintelligible.

Q8. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: The scientific community dismissed his radical new theory as pure ________, pointing out its blatant disregard for established laws of physics.
(A) genius
(B) pragmatism
(C) charlatanism
(D) orthodoxy

Answer: C
Explanation: If a theory disregards established laws, the community will reject it as fake. "Charlatanism" is the practice of making false claims to knowledge or skill (quackery).

Q9. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: The unrelenting heatwave ________ the agricultural output of the entire region, leading to massive food shortages.
(A) augmented
(B) decimated
(C) sustained
(D) galvanized

Answer: B
Explanation: A heatwave causing food shortages means the output was destroyed. "Decimated" means to kill, destroy, or remove a large percentage or part of.

Q10. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: Rather than offering a clear, definitive solution, the committee's final report was filled with ________ statements that confused everyone.
(A) equivocal
(B) lucid
(C) dogmatic
(D) categorical

Answer: A
Explanation: Statements that confuse rather than offer clear solutions are ambiguous. "Equivocal" means open to more than one interpretation; ambiguous.

Q11. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: Her ________ refusal to compromise on even the smallest details eventually alienated her most loyal supporters.
(A) flexible
(B) tractable
(C) intransigent
(D) accommodating

Answer: C
Explanation: Refusing to compromise characterizes stubbornness. "Intransigent" means unwilling or refusing to change one's views or to agree about something.

Q12. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: The seasoned detective immediately recognized the suspect's elaborate alibi as a cleverly constructed ________ designed to mislead the investigation.
(A) panacea
(B) subterfuge
(C) paradigm
(D) veracity

Answer: B
Explanation: An elaborate alibi designed to mislead is a trick. "Subterfuge" is deceit used in order to achieve one's goal.

Q13. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: Despite his outward display of arrogant confidence, he suffered from a deeply ________ sense of inadequacy that plagued his daily life.
(A) ephemeral
(B) peripheral
(C) ingrained
(D) ostentatious

Answer: C
Explanation: A sense of inadequacy that plagues daily life is deeply rooted. "Ingrained" (of a habit, belief, or attitude) means firmly fixed or established; difficult to change.

Q14. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: The corporation's decision to drastically cut employee healthcare benefits was met with ________ outrage from the labor union.
(A) tepid
(B) vociferous
(C) muted
(D) latent

Answer: B
Explanation: A drastic cut to benefits would result in loud, aggressive outrage. "Vociferous" (especially of a person or speech) means crying out noisily; clamorous.

15. Choose the correct word to fill in the blank: The relentless bombardment of sensationalized news has led to a state of collective ________, where the public is no longer shocked by genuine tragedies.
(A) empathy
(B) euphoria
(C) ennui
(D) perspicacity

Answer: C
Explanation: When the public is no longer shocked, they are suffering from apathy or boredom. "Ennui" refers to a feeling of listlessness and dissatisfaction arising from a lack of occupation or excitement (apathy/boredom).
