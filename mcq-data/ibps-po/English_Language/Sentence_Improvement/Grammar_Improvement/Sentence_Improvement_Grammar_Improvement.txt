Q1. Choose the grammatically correct sentence that improves upon the original.
Original: The committee have decided to unanimously reject the highly controversial proposal submitted by the urban development board last week.
(A) The committee has decided to unanimously reject the highly controversial proposal
(B) The committee have decided unanimously rejecting the highly controversial proposal
(C) The committee has decide to unanimously reject the highly controversial proposal
(D) The original sentence is correct.

Answer: A
Explanation: "Committee" is a collective noun. The word "unanimously" indicates they are acting as a single, undivided unit. Therefore, it must take the singular verb "has decided".

Q2. Choose the grammatically correct sentence that improves upon the original.
Original: No sooner he had entered the dimly lit room than the heavy oak door slammed shut behind him.
(A) No sooner did he entered the dimly lit room than
(B) No sooner had he entered the dimly lit room than
(C) No sooner he entered the dimly lit room when
(D) The original sentence is correct.

Answer: B
Explanation: When a sentence begins with "No sooner", it requires an inverted word order (auxiliary verb before the subject). "No sooner had he entered" is the correct grammatical structure.

Q3. Choose the grammatically correct sentence that improves upon the original.
Original: I would have definitely helped you complete the assignment if you would have asked me earlier.
(A) if you had asked me earlier.
(B) if you have asked me earlier.
(C) if you asked me earlier.
(D) The original sentence is correct.

Answer: A
Explanation: In a third conditional sentence (unreal past), the "if" clause must be in the past perfect tense ("if you had asked"), while the main clause uses "would have + past participle". "If you would have" is a common but incorrect phrasing.

Q4. Choose the grammatically correct sentence that improves upon the original.
Original: One of the most significant challenge faced by the developing nation is the severe lack of clean drinking water.
(A) One of the most significant challenges faced by the developing nation are
(B) One of the most significant challenges faced by the developing nation is
(C) One of the most significant challenge faced by the developing nations is
(D) The original sentence is correct.

Answer: B
Explanation: The phrase "One of the" is always followed by a plural noun ("challenges"), but because the actual subject is "One", the main verb must be singular ("is").

Q5. Choose the grammatically correct sentence that improves upon the original.
Original: Despite of his immense wealth and global influence, the eccentric billionaire lived a remarkably solitary and miserable life.
(A) In spite his immense wealth and global influence,
(B) Despite his immense wealth and global influence,
(C) Although of his immense wealth and global influence,
(D) The original sentence is correct.

Answer: B
Explanation: The preposition "despite" takes a noun or gerund directly without the preposition "of". ("In spite of" is correct, but "Despite of" is always wrong).

Q6. Choose the grammatically correct sentence that improves upon the original.
Original: The brilliant scientist, along with her entire team of researchers, were honored at the international gala.
(A) along with her entire team of researchers, are honored
(B) along with her entire team of researchers, was honored
(C) along with her entire team of researchers, have been honored
(D) The original sentence is correct.

Answer: B
Explanation: When subjects are connected by phrases like "along with", the verb must agree with the first subject ("The brilliant scientist", which is singular). Therefore, "was honored" is correct.

Q7. Choose the grammatically correct sentence that improves upon the original.
Original: Having finished the incredibly complex project, the manager allowed the exhausted team to leave early.
(A) Having finish the incredibly complex project,
(B) Have finished the incredibly complex project,
(C) After having finished the incredibly complex project,
(D) The original sentence is correct.

Answer: D
Explanation: "Having finished" is a perfect participle correctly indicating an action completed before the main verb's action. The sentence is grammatically flawless as written.

Q8. Choose the grammatically correct sentence that improves upon the original.
Original: He is not only an exceptionally talented musician but also plays tennis at a professional level.
(A) Not only is he an exceptionally talented musician but he also plays tennis
(B) He not only is an exceptionally talented musician but also plays tennis
(C) He is not only an exceptionally talented musician but also a professional tennis player.
(D) The original sentence is correct.

Answer: C
Explanation: Correlative conjunctions like "not only... but also" require parallel structure. In the original, "an exceptionally talented musician" (a noun phrase) is paired with "plays tennis" (a verb phrase). Option C perfectly balances two noun phrases.

Q9. Choose the grammatically correct sentence that improves upon the original.
Original: The number of cyberattacks targeting financial institutions have increased exponentially over the last decade.
(A) A number of cyberattacks targeting financial institutions has increased
(B) The number of cyberattacks targeting financial institutions has increased
(C) The numbers of cyberattacks targeting financial institutions have increased
(D) The original sentence is correct.

Answer: B
Explanation: The phrase "The number of" (referring to a specific mathematical quantity) always takes a singular verb ("has increased"), unlike "A number of", which takes a plural verb.

Q10. Choose the grammatically correct sentence that improves upon the original.
Original: Unless you do not apologize for your highly unprofessional behavior, the board will terminate your contract immediately.
(A) Unless you will not apologize for your highly unprofessional behavior,
(B) If you do not apologize for your highly unprofessional behavior,
(C) Unless you apologize for your highly unprofessional behavior,
(D) Both B and C are correct improvements.

Answer: D
Explanation: "Unless" inherently means "if not", so it cannot be followed by a negative word like "not" ("Unless you do not" is a double negative). Both "If you do not apologize" and "Unless you apologize" are grammatically correct and convey the identical meaning.

Q11. Choose the grammatically correct sentence that improves upon the original.
Original: The intricate details of the historical treaty is not fully understood by most modern political analysts.
(A) The intricate details of the historical treaty are not fully understood
(B) The intricate detail of the historical treaty are not fully understood
(C) The intricate details of the historical treaty has not fully understood
(D) The original sentence is correct.

Answer: A
Explanation: The true subject of the sentence is "details" (plural). "Of the historical treaty" is just a prepositional phrase modifying the subject. Therefore, the plural verb "are" must be used.

Q12. Choose the grammatically correct sentence that improves upon the original.
Original: I look forward to meet you at the upcoming global technology summit in San Francisco.
(A) I look forward to have met you at
(B) I look forward to meeting you at
(C) I am looking forward to meet you at
(D) The original sentence is correct.

Answer: B
Explanation: The phrase "look forward to" ends with a preposition ("to"), so the verb following it must be in the gerund form ("meeting").

Q13. Choose the grammatically correct sentence that improves upon the original.
Original: The newly constructed bridge is much more stronger than the older one that collapsed last year.
(A) is much stronger than the older one
(B) is more stronger than the older one
(C) is much more strong than the older one
(D) The original sentence is correct.

Answer: A
Explanation: "Stronger" is already in the comparative degree. Using "more" with it creates an incorrect double comparative ("more stronger"). "Much" can correctly modify a comparative ("much stronger").

14. Choose the grammatically correct sentence that improves upon the original.
Original: Neither the employees nor the department manager have been informed about the sudden policy changes.
(A) Neither the employees nor the department manager has been informed
(B) Neither the employee nor the department managers has been informed
(C) Neither the employees or the department manager have been informed
(D) The original sentence is correct.

Answer: A
Explanation: When using "neither...nor", the verb must agree with the subject closest to it. The closest subject is "manager" (singular), so the singular verb "has" must be used.

15. Choose the grammatically correct sentence that improves upon the original.
Original: It is high time the international community takes strict action against state-sponsored cyber terrorism.
(A) takes strictly action against
(B) took strict action against
(C) will take strict action against
(D) The original sentence is correct.

Answer: B
Explanation: The idiomatic phrase "It is high time" is followed by a clause in the simple past tense (subjunctive past) to express that an action is critically overdue. Therefore, "takes" must be improved to "took".
