Q1. Choose the grammatically correct sentence:
(A) He gave me a lot of informations about the project.
(B) He gave me many information about the project.
(C) He gave me much information about the project.
(D) He gave me a lots of informations about the project.

Answer: C
Explanation: "Information" is an uncountable noun. It does not take an 's' to become plural, and it is modified by "much" or "a lot of" rather than "many".

Q2. Choose the correct noun form to fill in the blank: The ________ of the Himalayas is truly breathtaking.
(A) sceneries
(B) scenery
(C) scene
(D) scenic

Answer: B
Explanation: "Scenery" is an uncountable noun and does not have a plural form ("sceneries" is incorrect). "Scene" refers to a specific incident or location, while "scenery" refers to the general landscape.

Q3. Choose the grammatically correct sentence:
(A) All the luggages were loaded onto the train.
(B) All the luggage were loaded onto the train.
(C) All the luggage was loaded onto the train.
(D) All the luggages was loaded onto the train.

Answer: C
Explanation: "Luggage" is an uncountable noun. It is always singular and takes a singular verb ("was").

Q4. Choose the correct noun to fill in the blank: The shepherd was grazing his ________ of sheep in the valley.
(A) herd
(B) pack
(C) flock
(D) swarm

Answer: C
Explanation: The collective noun used for a group of sheep is a "flock".

Q5. Choose the grammatically correct sentence:
(A) The committee are divided on this issue.
(B) The committee is divided on this issue.
(C) The committees is divided on this issue.
(D) The committee has divided on this issue.

Answer: A
Explanation: "Committee" is a collective noun. When the members act individually or are divided in opinion, it takes a plural verb ("are").

Q6. Choose the grammatically correct sentence:
(A) My trouser is too tight for me.
(B) My trousers is too tight for me.
(C) My trousers are too tight for me.
(D) My trouser are too tight for me.

Answer: C
Explanation: Certain nouns denoting garments consisting of two parts (trousers, scissors, spectacles) are always plural and take a plural verb.

Q7. Choose the correct noun phrase to fill in the blank: He bought two ________ from the market.
(A) dozens apple
(B) dozen apples
(C) dozens apples
(D) dozen apple

Answer: B
Explanation: When words like "dozen", "hundred", "thousand" are preceded by a definite numeral (like "two"), they do not take an 's' in the plural form. The noun following them is plural ("apples").

Q8. Choose the grammatically correct sentence:
(A) The measles have broken out in the city.
(B) The measles are broken out in the city.
(C) The measles has broken out in the city.
(D) The measle has broken out in the city.

Answer: C
Explanation: Names of certain diseases ending in 's' (like measles, mumps, rickets) are singular and take a singular verb ("has").

Q9. Choose the correct noun form to fill in the blank: The ________ of the old temple were discovered by the archeologists.
(A) remain
(B) remaining
(C) remains
(D) remained

Answer: C
Explanation: In this context, "remains" is a plural noun meaning the parts left over after other parts have been removed, used, or destroyed (historical ruins).

Corrected Q10: Choose the grammatically correct sentence:

Q11. Choose the grammatically correct sentence:
(A) The commander-in-chiefs held a meeting yesterday.
(B) The commanders-in-chief held a meeting yesterday.
(C) The commander-in-chief's held a meeting yesterday.
(D) The commanders-in-chiefs held a meeting yesterday.

Answer: B
Explanation: For compound nouns composed of a noun and a prepositional phrase, the plural is formed by adding 's' to the principal word (commander -> commanders).

Q12. Choose the correct noun to fill in the blank: The manager requires a dedicated workforce of ________ to complete the project on time.
(A) men servant
(B) man servants
(C) men servants
(D) man servant

Answer: C
Explanation: For compound nouns containing "man" or "woman" where both parts are principal, both parts must be pluralized (men servants, women doctors).

Q13. Choose the grammatically correct sentence:
(A) She has a five-rupees note in her pocket.
(B) She has a five-rupee notes in her pocket.
(C) She has a five-rupee note in her pocket.
(D) She has a five-rupees notes in her pocket.

Answer: C
Explanation: When a noun acts as an adjective modifying another noun (five-rupee modifying note), it remains in the singular form.

Q14. Choose the correct noun to fill in the blank: The poet's profound understanding of human emotions is evident in all his ________.
(A) poetry
(B) poetries
(C) poem
(D) poems

Answer: D
Explanation: "Poetry" is an uncountable noun referring to the art form and has no plural form. "Poems" is the correct countable plural. (He could have written many poems, representing his poetry).

15. Choose the grammatically correct sentence:
(A) The alumni of the university is gathering for the annual reunion.
(B) The alumnus of the university are gathering for the annual reunion.
(C) The alumni of the university are gathering for the annual reunion.
(D) The alumnuses of the university are gathering for the annual reunion.

Answer: C
Explanation: "Alumni" is the plural form of the Latin noun "alumnus" (a male graduate). Because it is plural, it takes the plural verb "are".
