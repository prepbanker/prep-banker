Q1. Choose the most appropriate logical connector to join the sentences.
Sentence 1: The city experienced unprecedented levels of rainfall this monsoon season.
Sentence 2: The municipal drainage systems completely collapsed, causing severe flooding.
(A) Nevertheless
(B) Consequently
(C) Conversely
(D) Likewise

Answer: B
Explanation: The collapse of the drainage systems is a direct result or consequence of the unprecedented rainfall. "Consequently" perfectly bridges the cause and effect.

Q2. Choose the most appropriate logical connector to join the sentences.
Sentence 1: He was severely reprimanded by the board of directors for his erratic behavior.
Sentence 2: He continued to act aggressively toward his subordinates.
(A) Therefore
(B) In addition
(C) Nevertheless
(D) Thus

Answer: C
Explanation: Being reprimanded should normally stop the bad behavior, but he continued anyway. "Nevertheless" denotes an unexpected contrast or concession.

Q3. Choose the most appropriate logical connector to join the sentences.
Sentence 1: You must strictly adhere to the project timeline outlined in the contract.
Sentence 2: The client will invoke the penalty clause and withhold payment.
(A) Furthermore
(B) Otherwise
(C) Hence
(D) Similarly

Answer: B
Explanation: The second sentence represents a negative alternative/consequence if the condition in the first sentence is not met. "Otherwise" expresses this condition correctly.

Q4. Choose the most appropriate logical connector to join the sentences.
Sentence 1: The novel is celebrated for its deep psychological exploration of grief.
Sentence 2: It is highly praised for its poetic and evocative prose.
(A) On the contrary
(B) Moreover
(C) Nevertheless
(D) Alternatively

Answer: B
Explanation: The second sentence adds another positive attribute to the first positive attribute. "Moreover" is used to add supplementary, supportive information.

Q5. Choose the most appropriate logical connector to join the sentences.
Sentence 1: The highway was heavily congested due to a massive multi-vehicle accident.
Sentence 2: We decided to take a scenic, winding detour through the mountains.
(A) Hence
(B) Despite this
(C) In addition
(D) Similarly

Answer: A
Explanation: Taking a detour was the logical result of the highway congestion. "Hence" means "as a consequence" or "for this reason".

Q6. Choose the most appropriate logical connector to join the sentences.
Sentence 1: The startup secured a massive injection of venture capital funding.
Sentence 2: They failed to scale their operations and ultimately filed for bankruptcy.
(A) Thus
(B) Conversely
(C) Yet
(D) Moreover

Answer: C
Explanation: Securing funding is a positive step toward success, but failing is a strong contrast to that expectation. "Yet" acts as a coordinating conjunction indicating this strong contrast.

Sentence 1: The new dietary regimen requires you to completely eliminate processed sugars.
Sentence 2: You are mandated to consume at least three liters of water daily.
(A) Rather
(B) Additionally
(C) Instead
(D) Consequently

Answer: B
Explanation: The second sentence adds another requirement to the regimen mentioned in the first. "Additionally" serves to append this supplementary rule.

Q8. Choose the most appropriate logical connector to join the sentences.
Sentence 1: The socio-political climate in the region is incredibly volatile and hostile.
Sentence 2: A small, resilient community of artists continues to thrive there.
(A) Accordingly
(B) Even so
(C) Similarly
(D) Subsequently

Answer: B
Explanation: A hostile climate usually prevents communities from thriving, yet they thrive. "Even so" means "in spite of that" and introduces the surprising contrast.

Q9. Choose the most appropriate logical connector to join the sentences.
Sentence 1: The rapid automation of rudimentary manufacturing processes has displaced thousands of factory workers.
Sentence 2: It has inadvertently catalyzed a massive demand for highly skilled robotic engineers.
(A) Simultaneously
(B) Otherwise
(C) To conclude
(D) On the contrary

Answer: A
Explanation: The two effects of automation (displacing workers and creating demand for engineers) are happening at the same time. "Simultaneously" correctly connects these parallel events.

Q10. Choose the most appropriate logical connector to join the sentences.
Sentence 1: He lacked the traditional academic pedigree usually required for such an elite position.
Sentence 2: His vast, unparalleled practical experience in crisis management made him the ideal candidate.
(A) Because
(B) Henceforth
(C) On the other hand
(D) Subsequently

Answer: C
Explanation: The two sentences present two contrasting perspectives on his suitability for the job. "On the other hand" is used to introduce a contrasting point of view or fact.

Q11. Choose the most appropriate logical connector to join the sentences.
Sentence 1: The central banking authority aggressively slashed interest rates to near zero.
Sentence 2: The national inflation rate soared to an unprecedented, alarming high.
(A) Provided that
(B) Subsequent to
(C) As a corollary
(D) In spite of this

Answer: C
Explanation: "As a corollary" means as a direct or natural consequence or result. The soaring inflation is a direct consequence of slashing interest rates.

Q12. Choose the most appropriate logical connector to join the sentences.
Sentence 1: The proposed corporate merger was incredibly lucrative and beneficial for both firms on paper.
Sentence 2: The regulatory board vetoed the acquisition over severe antitrust concerns.
(A) Accordingly
(B) Ergo
(C) Notwithstanding
(D) Albeit

Answer: C
Explanation: The merger was highly beneficial, but it was stopped anyway. "Notwithstanding" means "in spite of this" or "nevertheless", which correctly bridges the contrast.

Q13. Choose the most appropriate logical connector to join the sentences.
Sentence 1: The initial phase of the clinical trials demonstrated an astonishing 95% efficacy rate against the pathogen.
Sentence 2: Subsequent, long-term testing revealed severe, irreversible neurological side effects.
(A) Consequently
(B) Conversely
(C) Similarly
(D) Inherently

Answer: B
Explanation: The first sentence is highly positive. The second sentence introduces a completely negative opposite finding. "Conversely" introduces a statement or idea which reverses one that has just been made.

Q14. Choose the most appropriate logical connector to join the sentences.
Sentence 1: Stringent, military-grade cybersecurity protocols were implemented across the corporate network.
Sentence 2: A sophisticated state-sponsored hack compromised millions of highly classified documents.
(A) Ergo
(B) Henceforth
(C) Nonetheless
(D) Thus

Answer: C
Explanation: Despite strict protocols being in place, a massive breach still occurred. "Nonetheless" means "in spite of that", serving as the perfect contrast connector.

15. Choose the most appropriate logical connector to join the sentences.
Sentence 1: The ancient parchment is heavily fragmented, burned at the edges, and severely faded.
Sentence 2: Linguistic scholars have remarkably managed to decipher the core theological arguments hidden within it.
(A) Given that
(B) Ergo
(C) In contrast
(D) Be that as it may

Answer: D
Explanation: The manuscript is badly damaged (a negative fact). However, scholars still deciphered it. "Be that as it may" means "despite that fact" or "accepting that that is true", which perfectly links the concession to the achievement.
