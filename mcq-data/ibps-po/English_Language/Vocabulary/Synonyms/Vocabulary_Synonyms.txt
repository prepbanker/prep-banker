Subtopic: Synonyms

Q1. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **AMICABLE**
(A) Hostile
(B) Friendly
(C) Jealous
(D) Indifferent

Answer: B
Explanation: "Amicable" means characterized by friendliness and absence of discord. "Friendly" is its exact synonym.

Q2. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **BRIEF**
(A) Lengthy
(B) Concise
(C) Detailed
(D) Massive

Answer: B
Explanation: "Brief" means of short duration or concise in expression. "Concise" is the exact synonym.

Q3. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **FEEBLE**
(A) Strong
(B) Weak
(C) Brave
(D) Sturdy

Answer: B
Explanation: "Feeble" means lacking physical strength, especially as a result of age or illness. "Weak" is the exact synonym.

Q4. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **ABUNDANT**
(A) Scarce
(B) Plentiful
(C) Rare
(D) Depleted

Answer: B
Explanation: "Abundant" means existing or available in large quantities. "Plentiful" is its exact synonym.

Q5. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **DEFER**
(A) Accelerate
(B) Postpone
(C) Cancel
(D) Accomplish

Answer: B
Explanation: "Defer" means to put off (an action or event) to a later time. "Postpone" is the exact synonym.

Q6. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **LUCID**
(A) Confusing
(B) Opaque
(C) Clear
(D) Muddy

Answer: C
Explanation: "Lucid" means expressed clearly or easy to understand. "Clear" is the exact synonym.

Q7. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **VINDICATE**
(A) Accuse
(B) Justify
(C) Condemn
(D) Ignore

Answer: B
Explanation: "Vindicate" means to clear someone of blame or suspicion, or to show or prove to be right, reasonable, or justified. "Justify" is the closest synonym.

Q8. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **METICULOUS**
(A) Careless
(B) Hasty
(C) Fastidious
(D) Sloppy

Answer: C
Explanation: "Meticulous" means showing great attention to detail; very careful and precise. "Fastidious" is the closest synonym.

Q9. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **DILIGENT**
(A) Lazy
(B) Assiduous
(C) Lethargic
(D) Indolent

Answer: B
Explanation: "Diligent" means having or showing care and conscientiousness in one's work. "Assiduous" is the exact synonym.

Q10. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **EPHEMERAL**
(A) Permanent
(B) Transient
(C) Eternal
(D) Lasting

Answer: B
Explanation: "Ephemeral" means lasting for a very short time. "Transient" is the exact synonym.

Q11. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **OBSEQUIOUS**
(A) Domineering
(B) Arrogant
(C) Sycophantic
(D) Assertive

Answer: C
Explanation: "Obsequious" means obedient or attentive to an excessive or servile degree. "Sycophantic" is the exact synonym.

Q12. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **PERNICIOUS**
(A) Beneficial
(B) Deleterious
(C) Salutary
(D) Innocent

Answer: B
Explanation: "Pernicious" means having a harmful effect, especially in a gradual or subtle way. "Deleterious" (causing harm or damage) is its exact synonym.

Q13. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **ESOTERIC**
(A) Common
(B) Abstruse
(C) Familiar
(D) Obvious

Answer: B
Explanation: "Esoteric" means intended for or likely to be understood by only a small number of people with a specialized knowledge. "Abstruse" (difficult to understand) is the closest synonym.

Q14. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **TRUCULENT**
(A) Amiable
(B) Peaceful
(C) Pugnacious
(D) Docile

Answer: C
Explanation: "Truculent" means eager or quick to argue or fight. "Pugnacious" is its exact synonym.

15. Choose the exact synonym for the word: **CACOPHONY**
(A) Harmony
(B) Dissonance
(C) Melody
(D) Symphony

Answer: B
Explanation: "Cacophony" refers to a harsh, discordant mixture of sounds. "Dissonance" is the exact synonym.
