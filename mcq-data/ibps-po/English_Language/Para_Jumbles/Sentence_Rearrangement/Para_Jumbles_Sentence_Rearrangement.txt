Q1. Rearrange the following parts (P, Q, R, S) to form a meaningful sentence:
P: the catastrophic failure of the dam
Q: due to unprecedented torrential rains
R: resulted in massive flash floods
S: that completely submerged the downstream villages

(A) PQRS
(B) QPRS
(C) PRQS
(D) RQPS

Answer: A
Explanation: P introduces the subject (catastrophic failure of the dam). Q provides the reason (due to torrential rains). R gives the immediate consequence (resulted in massive flash floods). S describes the extent of the floods (that completely submerged...). The flow is Subject -> Reason -> Result -> Extent. (Wait, "The catastrophic failure of the dam due to unprecedented torrential rains resulted in massive flash floods that completely submerged the downstream villages." - PQRS flows perfectly).

Q2. Rearrange the following parts (P, Q, R, S) to form a meaningful sentence:
P: to effectively mitigate the devastating impacts
Q: implementing stringent carbon emission regulations
R: of accelerating global climate change
S: is now an absolute global imperative

(A) QPSR
(B) QSPR
(C) SQPR
(D) PRSQ

Answer: B
Explanation: Q introduces the main subject (implementing stringent carbon emission regulations). S is the predicate (is now an absolute global imperative). P introduces the purpose (to effectively mitigate the devastating impacts). R completes P (of accelerating global climate change). So the sequence is Q-S-P-R. "Implementing stringent carbon emission regulations is now an absolute global imperative to effectively mitigate the devastating impacts of accelerating global climate change."
