Q1. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, ?
(A) 45
(B) 47
(C) 49
(D) 51

Answer: C
Explanation:
The series consists of the squares of consecutive natural numbers.
1^2 = 1
2^2 = 4
3^2 = 9
4^2 = 16
5^2 = 25
6^2 = 36
7^2 = 49

Q9. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
14, 27, 52, 101, 198, ?
(A) 381
(B) 391
(C) 395
(D) 405

Answer: C
Explanation:
The pattern involves multiplying by 2 and then alternatively subtracting the squares of consecutive integers.
14 x 2 - 1^2 = 28 - 1 = 27
27 x 2 - 2^2 = 54 - 4 = 52
52 x 2 - 3^2 = 104 - 9 = 95 (Wait, 95 is not 101).
Let's find the correct pattern.
Differences:
27 - 14 = 13
52 - 27 = 25
101 - 52 = 49
198 - 101 = 97
Second differences:
25 - 13 = 12
49 - 25 = 24
97 - 49 = 48
This is geometric difference! Let's rewrite Q9 to fit Square Patterns.

Q11. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?

Explanation:

15. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
10, 11, 15, 31, 80, ?
(A) 211
(B) 222
(C) 224
(D) 236

Answer: C
Explanation:
Let's check the differences between the numbers.
11 - 10 = 1
15 - 11 = 4
31 - 15 = 16
80 - 31 = 49
The differences are 1, 4, 16, 49.
Notice that these are squares: 1^2, 2^2, 4^2, 7^2.
The sequence of the bases being squared is: 1, 2, 4, 7...
The difference between the bases is:
2 - 1 = 1
4 - 2 = 2
7 - 4 = 3
So the next base should be 7 + 4 = 11.
The next difference in the main series will be 11^2 = 121.
Next term = 80 + 121 = 201.
Wait, 201 is not in the options. Let's rewrite the differences to be simpler.
Let's do differences of: 1^2, 2^2, 3^2, 4^2... no, that's too easy for Q15.
Let's use squares of prime numbers: 2^2, 3^2, 5^2, 7^2, 11^2.
Corrected 15:
10, 14, 23, 48, 97, ?
Differences:
14 - 10 = 4 (2^2)
23 - 14 = 9 (3^2)
48 - 23 = 25 (5^2)
97 - 48 = 49 (7^2)
The differences are squares of consecutive prime numbers.
The next prime number is 11.
Next difference = 11^2 = 121.
Next term = 97 + 121 = 218.

Let's write this cleanly:
