Q1. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
3, 6, 12, 24, 48, ?
(A) 84
(B) 90
(C) 96
(D) 102

Answer: C
Explanation:
The series follows a constant multiplication pattern of x2.
3 x 2 = 6
6 x 2 = 12
12 x 2 = 24
24 x 2 = 48
48 x 2 = 96

Q3. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
1280, 640, 320, 160, 80, ?

(B) 30

80 ÷ 2 = 40

Q10. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
6, 9, 27, 135, ?, 6750
(A) 745
(B) 875
(C) 945
(D) 1025

Answer: C
Explanation:
The multiplier follows a sequence of odd numbers.
6 x 1.5 = 9
9 x 3 = 27
27 x 5 = 135
135 x 7 = 945
945 x 9 = 8505. Wait, 945 x 9 is not 6750.
Let's re-evaluate the multipliers:
6 x 1.5 = 9
9 x 3 = 27
27 x 5 = 135
Let's check the sequence of multipliers: 1.5, 3, 5, ... this is not consistent.
Let's try:
6 x 1.5 = 9
9 x 3 = 27
27 x 5 is wrong. 27 x 5 = 135. Yes.
Multipliers: 1.5, 3, 5, ...
Difference of multipliers: 3 - 1.5 = 1.5.
5 - 3 = 2.0.
Next difference = 2.5? So 5 + 2.5 = 7.5.
135 x 7.5 = 1012.5. Doesn't match options.
Let's try another geometric logic.
6 x 1.5 = 9
9 x 3 (which is 1.5 x 2) = 27
27 x 5 (wait, 1.5, 3, 5? Maybe it's 1.5, 3.0, 5.0? No).
Let's rewrite Q10 with a solid geometric pattern.

Corrected Q10:
Q10. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
7, 14, 42, 168, 840, ?
(A) 4800
(B) 5040
(C) 5240
(D) 5400

Answer: B
Explanation:
The multiplier is increasing by consecutive integers starting from 2.
7 x 2 = 14
14 x 3 = 42
42 x 4 = 168
168 x 5 = 840
840 x 6 = 5040

Q12. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
12, 12, 18, 45, 180, ?
(A) 1040
(B) 1080
(C) 1120
(D) 1160

Answer: B
Explanation:
Let's check the multipliers:
12 x 1 = 12
12 x 1.5 = 18
18 x 2.5 = 45
45 x 4 = 180
Multipliers are: 1, 1.5, 2.5, 4.
Differences of multipliers: 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2.
So the next multiplier = 4 + 2 = 6.
180 x 6 = 1080.

Q14. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
2048, 512, 256, 128, 128, ?
(A) 128
(B) 192
(C) 256
(D) 512

Answer: C
Explanation:
Let's check the division/multiplication factors:
2048 / 4 = 512 (or x 0.25)
512 / 2 = 256 (or x 0.5)
256 / 2 = 128 (or x 0.5) -- wait, 0.25, 0.5, 0.5?
Let's rewrite the logic for Q14.
Corrected Q14:
4096, 512, 128, 64, 64, ?
Factors:
4096 ÷ 8 = 512
512 ÷ 4 = 128
128 ÷ 2 = 64
64 ÷ 1 = 64
64 ÷ 0.5 = 128.
Let's frame this.
Q14. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
4096, 512, 128, 64, 64, ?
(A) 64
(B) 96
(C) 128
(D) 256

Answer: C
Explanation:
The series follows a division pattern where the divisor halves at each step.
4096 ÷ 8 = 512
512 ÷ 4 = 128
128 ÷ 2 = 64
64 ÷ 1 = 64
64 ÷ 0.5 = 128 (Dividing by 0.5 is equivalent to multiplying by 2).

15. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
1, 1, 2, 6, 24, 120, 720, ?
(A) 4040
(B) 5040
(C) 6040
(D) 7040

Answer: B
Explanation:
The series represents factorials, or multiplying by consecutive integers.
1 x 1 = 1 (1!)
1 x 2 = 2 (2!)
2 x 3 = 6 (3!)
6 x 4 = 24 (4!)
24 x 5 = 120 (5!)
120 x 6 = 720 (6!)
720 x 7 = 5040 (7!)
