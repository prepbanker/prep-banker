Q1. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
1, 8, 27, 64, 125, ?
(A) 196
(B) 216
(C) 256
(D) 343

Answer: B
Explanation:
The series consists of the cubes of consecutive natural numbers.
1^3 = 1
2^3 = 8
3^3 = 27
4^3 = 64
5^3 = 125
6^3 = 216

Q9. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
15, 23, 50, 114, 239, ?
(A) 445
(B) 455
(C) 465
(D) 475

Answer: B
Explanation:
Let's find the differences between consecutive terms.
23 - 15 = 8 (2^3)
50 - 23 = 27 (3^3)
114 - 50 = 64 (4^3)
239 - 114 = 125 (5^3)
The differences are the cubes of consecutive integers starting from 2.
Next difference = 6^3 = 216.
Next term = 239 + 216 = 455.

Q11. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?

(D) 15

Answer: A
Explanation:

200 - 1 = 199 (1^3)
199 - 8 = 191 (2^3)

The differences are the cubes of consecutive numbers subtracted from the previous term.
Next subtraction = 5^3 = 125.
Next term = 100 - 125 = -25.

Q12. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
2, 12, 36, 80, 150, ?
(A) 248
(B) 252
(C) 258
(D) 262

Answer: B
Explanation:
The series follows the pattern of (n^3 + n^2).
1^3 + 1^2 = 1 + 1 = 2
2^3 + 2^2 = 8 + 4 = 12
3^3 + 3^2 = 27 + 9 = 36
4^3 + 4^2 = 64 + 16 = 80
5^3 + 5^2 = 125 + 25 = 150
6^3 + 6^2 = 216 + 36 = 252

Q13. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
0, 4, 18, 48, 100, ?
(A) 160
(B) 170
(C) 180
(D) 190

Answer: C
Explanation:

1^3 - 1^2 = 1 - 1 = 0
2^3 - 2^2 = 8 - 4 = 4
3^3 - 3^2 = 27 - 9 = 18
4^3 - 4^2 = 64 - 16 = 48
5^3 - 5^2 = 125 - 25 = 100
6^3 - 6^2 = 216 - 36 = 180
