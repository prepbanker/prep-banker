Q1. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
12, 17, 22, 27, 32, ?
(A) 35
(B) 37
(C) 39
(D) 42

Answer: B
Explanation:
The series follows a constant addition pattern of +5.
12 + 5 = 17
17 + 5 = 22
22 + 5 = 27
27 + 5 = 32
32 + 5 = 37

Q2. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
85, 78, 71, 64, 57, ?
(A) 49
(B) 50
(C) 52
(D) 54

Answer: B
Explanation:
The series follows a constant subtraction pattern of -7.
85 - 7 = 78
78 - 7 = 71
71 - 7 = 64
64 - 7 = 57
57 - 7 = 50

Q6. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
15, 17, 21, 27, 35, ?
(A) 43
(B) 45
(C) 47
(D) 49

Answer: B
Explanation:
The difference between consecutive terms is increasing by 2 (+2, +4, +6, +8...).
15 + 2 = 17
17 + 4 = 21
21 + 6 = 27
27 + 8 = 35
35 + 10 = 45

Q7. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
90, 87, 81, 72, 60, ?
(A) 43
(B) 45
(C) 47
(D) 49

Answer: B
Explanation:
The difference between consecutive terms is decreasing progressively (-3, -6, -9, -12...).
90 - 3 = 87
87 - 6 = 81
81 - 9 = 72
72 - 12 = 60
60 - 15 = 45

Q8. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
8, 11, 17, 26, 38, ?
(A) 51
(B) 53
(C) 55
(D) 57

Answer: B
Explanation:
The difference between consecutive terms follows the pattern of odd numbers starting from 3 (+3, +6, +9, +12...). Wait, 17-11=6. Let's check differences:
11 - 8 = 3
17 - 11 = 6
26 - 17 = 9
38 - 26 = 12
The differences are multiples of 3 (3, 6, 9, 12, 15).
Next difference = 15.
38 + 15 = 53.

Q12. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
112, 108, 99, 83, 58, ?
(A) 18
(B) 22
(C) 26
(D) 28

Answer: B
Explanation:
Let's check the differences:
112 - 108 = 4
108 - 99 = 9
99 - 83 = 16
83 - 58 = 25
The differences are consecutive perfect squares (2^2, 3^2, 4^2, 5^2, 6^2).
Next difference = 6^2 = 36.
Next term = 58 - 36 = 22.

Q13. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
4, 9, 28, 69, 144, ?
(A) 249
(B) 259
(C) 269
(D) 279

Answer: C
Explanation:
Let's find the first differences:
9 - 4 = 5
28 - 9 = 19
69 - 28 = 41
144 - 69 = 75
Now let's find the second differences:
19 - 5 = 14
41 - 19 = 22
75 - 41 = 34
Now let's find the third differences:
22 - 14 = 8
34 - 22 = 12
If the third difference increases by 4 (8, 12, 16):
Next second difference = 34 + 16 = 50.
Next first difference = 75 + 50 = 125.
Next term = 144 + 125 = 269.

Q14. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
17, 19, 25, 37, 57, ?
(A) 85
(B) 87
(C) 89
(D) 91

Answer: B
Explanation:
Let's find the differences:
19 - 17 = 2
25 - 19 = 6
37 - 25 = 12
57 - 37 = 20
These differences (2, 6, 12, 20) follow the pattern of n^2 + n (or 1x2, 2x3, 3x4, 4x5).
1^2 + 1 = 2
2^2 + 2 = 6
3^2 + 3 = 12
4^2 + 4 = 20
Next difference should be 5^2 + 5 = 25 + 5 = 30. (5x6 = 30).
Next term = 57 + 30 = 87.

15. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
8, 15, 36, 78, 148, ?
(A) 251
(B) 253
(C) 255
(D) 257

Answer: B
Explanation:
Let's find the first differences:
15 - 8 = 7
36 - 15 = 21
78 - 36 = 42
148 - 78 = 70
Now let's find the second differences:
21 - 7 = 14
42 - 21 = 21
70 - 42 = 28
The second differences are multiples of 7 (14, 21, 28, 35).
Next second difference = 35.
Next first difference = 70 + 35 = 105.
Next term = 148 + 105 = 253.
