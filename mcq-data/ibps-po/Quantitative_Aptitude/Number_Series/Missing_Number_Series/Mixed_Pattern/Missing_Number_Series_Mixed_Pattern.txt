Q1. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
4, 5, 12, 39, 160, ?
(A) 795
(B) 805
(C) 815
(D) 825

Answer: B
Explanation:
The series follows a multiplication and addition pattern.
4 x 1 + 1 = 5
5 x 2 + 2 = 12
12 x 3 + 3 = 39
39 x 4 + 4 = 160
160 x 5 + 5 = 805

15. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following number series?
4, 18, 85, 336, 1005, ?
(A) 2008
(B) 2018
(C) 2028
(D) 2038

Answer: A
Explanation:
Let's find the pattern. This looks like a descending multiplier.
4 x 5 - 2 = 18
18 x 4 + 13 = 85 (No).
Let's try:
4 x 4 + 2 = 18
18 x 5 - 5 = 85 (No).
Let's try:
4 x 6 - 6 = 18
18 x 5 - 5 = 85
85 x 4 - 4 = 340 - 4 = 336
336 x 3 - 3 = 1008 - 3 = 1005
The multiplier and subtractor are decreasing consecutively.
Next term = 1005 x 2 - 2 = 2010 - 2 = 2008.
