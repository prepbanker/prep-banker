Q1. If 30% of a number is 120, what is 75% of that number?
(A) 250
(B) 300
(C) 350
(D) 400

Answer: B
Explanation:
Let the number be 'x'.
30% of x = 120
(30/100) * x = 120
x = (120 * 100) / 30 = 400
Now, 75% of x = (75/100) * 400 = 75 * 4 = 300.

Q2. The price of an article is first increased by 20% and then decreased by 10%. What is the net percentage change in the price?
(A) 8% increase
(B) 10% increase
(C) 8% decrease
(D) 12% increase

Answer: A
Explanation:
Let the initial price be 100.
After 20% increase, price = 100 + 20 = 120.
After 10% decrease on 120, decrease = 10% of 120 = 12.
New price = 120 - 12 = 108.
Net change = 108 - 100 = +8.
So, the net percentage change is an 8% increase.
Alternatively, use formula: A + B + (AB/100) = 20 - 10 + (20*-10)/100 = 10 - 2 = 8% increase.

Q3. In an election between two candidates, the winner got 60% of the total valid votes and won by a majority of 2400 votes. If there were no invalid votes, what is the total number of votes polled?
(A) 8000
(B) 10000
(C) 12000
(D) 14000

Answer: C
Explanation:
Let the total votes be 100%.
Winner got 60%, so the loser got (100% - 60%) = 40%.
Majority difference = 60% - 40% = 20%.
Given, 20% of total votes = 2400.
Total votes (100%) = (2400 / 20) * 100 = 12000.

Q4. If A's salary is 25% more than B's salary, then by what percentage is B's salary less than A's salary?
(A) 15%
(B) 20%
(C) 25%
(D) 33.33%

Answer: B
Explanation:
Let B's salary be 100.
Then, A's salary = 100 + 25 = 125.
Difference = 125 - 100 = 25.
Percentage by which B's salary is less than A's = (Difference / A's salary) * 100
= (25 / 125) * 100
= (1 / 5) * 100 = 20%.

Q5. A student has to secure 40% marks to pass an examination. He gets 180 marks and fails by 20 marks. Find the maximum marks of the examination.
(A) 400
(B) 450
(C) 500
(D) 600

Answer: C
Explanation:
Passing marks = Marks obtained + Marks failed by
Passing marks = 180 + 20 = 200.
Given that the passing marks are 40% of the maximum marks.
Let maximum marks be 'M'.
40% of M = 200
(40/100) * M = 200
M = (200 * 100) / 40 = 500.

Q6. In a town, 65% of the population reads English newspaper, 45% reads Hindi newspaper, and 20% reads both. What percentage of the population does not read either of the two newspapers?
(A) 5%
(B) 10%
(C) 15%
(D) 20%

Answer: B
Explanation:
Let the total population be 100%.
Percentage reading at least one newspaper = (Read English) + (Read Hindi) - (Read Both)
= 65% + 45% - 20%
= 110% - 20% = 90%.
Percentage reading neither = 100% - 90% = 10%.

Q7. The price of petrol increases by 25%. By what percentage must a person reduce his consumption so that his expenditure on petrol remains the same?
(A) 15%
(B) 20%
(C) 25%
(D) 30%

Answer: B
Explanation:
Let original price be Rs. 100/liter and consumption be 100 liters.
Original expenditure = 100 * 100 = 10000.
New price = Rs. 125/liter. Let new consumption be 'C' liters.
New expenditure = 125 * C = 10000.
C = 10000 / 125 = 80 liters.
Reduction in consumption = 100 - 80 = 20 liters.
Percentage reduction = (20 / 100) * 100 = 20%.
Shortcut: (R / (100+R)) * 100 = (25 / 125) * 100 = 20%.

Q8. A man spends 30% of his income on food, 20% of the remaining on rent, and 25% of the still remaining on education. If he saves Rs. 4200, what is his total income?
(A) Rs. 8000
(B) Rs. 10000
(C) Rs. 12000
(D) Rs. 15000

Answer: B
Explanation:
Let his total income be 'x'.
After spending 30% on food, remaining = 70% of x = 0.7x.
After spending 20% of remaining on rent, new remaining = 80% of 0.7x = 0.56x.
After spending 25% of the rest on education, final remaining (savings) = 75% of 0.56x.
0.75 * 0.56x = 4200
(3/4) * (56/100) * x = 4200
(42/100) * x = 4200
x = (4200 * 100) / 42 = 10000.

Q9. In an examination, a candidate got 30% marks and failed by 30 marks. Another candidate got 45% marks and got 15 marks more than the minimum pass marks. Find the passing percentage.
(A) 35%
(B) 38%
(C) 40%
(D) 42%

Answer: C
Explanation:
Let total maximum marks be 'M'.
Passing marks = 30% of M + 30
Passing marks = 45% of M - 15
Equating the two:
(30/100)M + 30 = (45/100)M - 15
(15/100)M = 45
M = (45 * 100) / 15 = 300.
Passing marks = 30% of 300 + 30 = 90 + 30 = 120.
Passing percentage = (120 / 300) * 100 = 40%.

Q10. The population of a city increases by 10% annually. If the present population is 1,33,100, what was the population 3 years ago?
(A) 80,000
(B) 90,000
(C) 1,00,000
(D) 1,10,000

Answer: C
Explanation:
Let the population 3 years ago be 'P'.
Present Population = P * (1 + R/100)^n
1,33,100 = P * (1 + 10/100)^3
1,33,100 = P * (11/10)^3
1,33,100 = P * (1331 / 1000)
P = (1,33,100 * 1000) / 1331
P = 100 * 1000 = 1,00,000.

Q11. Fresh fruit contains 68% water and dry fruit contains 20% water. How much dry fruit can be obtained from 100 kg of fresh fruits?
(A) 32 kg
(B) 40 kg
(C) 48 kg
(D) 52 kg

Answer: B
Explanation:
The quantity of fruit pulp remains constant whether the fruit is fresh or dry.
In 100 kg of fresh fruit, water = 68%, so pulp = 32% of 100 kg = 32 kg.
Let the total weight of dry fruit obtained be 'x' kg.
In dry fruit, water = 20%, so pulp = 80% of 'x'.
Since pulp quantity is constant:
80% of x = 32
(80/100) * x = 32
x = (32 * 100) / 80 = 40 kg.

Q12. A solution of salt and water contains 5% salt by weight. If 20 liters of water evaporates, the salt concentration increases to 15%. Find the original volume of the solution.
(A) 25 liters
(B) 30 liters
(C) 35 liters
(D) 40 liters

Answer: B
Explanation:
Let the original volume of the solution be 'V' liters.
Amount of salt initially = 5% of V = 0.05V.
When 20 liters of water evaporates, the new volume is (V - 20) liters.
The amount of salt remains the same, but it is now 15% of the new volume.
15% of (V - 20) = 0.05V
0.15(V - 20) = 0.05V
0.15V - 3 = 0.05V
0.10V = 3
V = 3 / 0.10 = 30 liters.

Q13. In a class, 70% of the students passed in Mathematics, 80% passed in Science, and 10% failed in both subjects. If 144 students passed in both subjects, what is the total number of students in the class?
(A) 180
(B) 240
(C) 300
(D) 360

Answer: B
Explanation:
Let total students be 100%.
Failed in both = 10%. Thus, students who passed in at least one subject = 100% - 10% = 90%.
Passed in Math (M) = 70%, Passed in Science (S) = 80%.
n(M U S) = n(M) + n(S) - n(M ∩ S)
90% = 70% + 80% - n(M ∩ S)
n(M ∩ S) = 150% - 90% = 60%.
So, 60% of students passed in both subjects.
Given that 60% of Total = 144.
Total students = (144 / 60) * 100 = 240.

Q14. An employer reduces the number of his employees in the ratio 9:8 and increases their wages in the ratio 14:15. If the original total wage bill was Rs. 18,900, what is the new total wage bill?
(A) Rs. 17,500
(B) Rs. 18,000
(C) Rs. 18,500
(D) Rs. 19,000

Answer: B
Explanation:
Let the initial number of employees be 9x and the new number be 8x.
Let the initial wage per employee be 14y and the new wage be 15y.
Initial total wage bill = 9x * 14y = 126xy.
New total wage bill = 8x * 15y = 120xy.
We know that 126xy = 18,900.
xy = 18900 / 126 = 150.
New wage bill = 120 * 150 = 18,000.

15. Mr. X gave 40% of his money to his wife, 20% of the remaining to his son, and half of the remaining to his daughter. After this, he donated 50% of what was left and finally had Rs. 12,000 remaining. How much money did he have initially?
(A) Rs. 80,000
(B) Rs. 1,00,000
(C) Rs. 1,20,000
(D) Rs. 1,50,000

Answer: B
Explanation:
Let initial money be M.
After giving 40% to wife, remaining = 60% of M = 0.6M.
Gave 20% of remaining to son, new remaining = 80% of 0.6M = 0.48M.
Gave half (50%) of remaining to daughter, new remaining = 50% of 0.48M = 0.24M.
Donated 50% of the rest, final remaining = 50% of 0.24M = 0.12M.
0.12M = 12,000
M = 12,000 / 0.12 = 1,00,000.
