Directions (Q1-Q5): Study the given pie chart carefully and answer the following questions.
Refer to diagram: SVG/Q1.svg
The pie chart shows the percentage distribution of 2500 students in 5 different schools (A, B, C, D, E).

Q1. What is the total number of students in School A and School C together?
(A) 900
(B) 950
(C) 1000
(D) 1050
Answer: B
Explanation: 
Percentage in A = 20%.
Percentage in C = 18%.
Total percentage = 38%.
Total students = 38% of 2500 = 0.38 * 2500 = 950.

Q2. The number of students in School D is what percent more or less than the number of students in School B?
(A) 20% more
(B) 25% more
(C) 20% less
(D) 25% less
Answer: A
Explanation: 
Percentage in D = 30%.
Percentage in B = 25%.
Difference = 5%.
% more = (5 / 25) * 100 = 20% more.

Q3. What is the central angle corresponding to School E?
(A) 25.2°
(B) 28.8°
(C) 32.4°
(D) 36.0°
Answer: A
Explanation: 
Percentage in E = 7%. (Since A=20, B=25, C=18, D=30. Total = 93. So E = 7%).
Central angle = 7% of 360° = (7 / 100) * 360 = 25.2°.

Q4. Find the ratio of the number of students in School A to the number of students in School E.
(A) 15 : 7
(B) 20 : 7
(C) 25 : 7
(D) 30 : 7
Answer: B
Explanation: 
Percentage in A = 20%.
Percentage in E = 7%.
Ratio of students = Ratio of percentages = 20 : 7.

Q5. If 40% of the students in School D are girls, find the number of boys in School D.
(A) 400
(B) 450
(C) 500
(D) 550
Answer: B
Explanation: 
Total students in D = 30% of 2500 = 750.
Girls = 40%, so boys = 60%.
Number of boys = 60% of 750 = 0.6 * 750 = 450.

Directions (Q6-Q10): Study the given pie chart carefully and answer the following questions.
Refer to diagram: SVG/Q6.svg
The pie chart shows the percentage distribution of monthly expenses of a family on various items. Total monthly income is Rs. 40,000.
(Rent: 22%, Food: 35%, Education: 15%, Travel: 10%, Savings: 18%)

Q6. What is the amount spent by the family on Food?
(A) Rs. 12000
(B) Rs. 13000
(C) Rs. 14000
(D) Rs. 15000
Answer: C
Explanation: 
Amount on Food = 35% of 40000 = 0.35 * 40000 = 14000.

Q7. The amount spent on Rent and Travel together is equal to what fraction of the total income?
(A) 7/25
(B) 8/25
(C) 9/25
(D) 2/5
Answer: B
Explanation: 
Rent = 22%, Travel = 10%.
Total = 32%.
Fraction = 32 / 100 = 8 / 25.

Q8. How much more does the family spend on Education than on Travel?
(A) Rs. 1500
(B) Rs. 2000
(C) Rs. 2500
(D) Rs. 3000
Answer: B
Explanation: 
Education = 15%, Travel = 10%.
Difference = 5%.
Amount = 5% of 40000 = 2000.

Q9. The central angle for the sector representing Savings is:
(A) 54.8°
(B) 60.5°
(C) 64.8°
(D) 72.0°
Answer: C
Explanation: 
Savings = 18%.
Central angle = 18% of 360° = 18 * 3.6 = 64.8°.

Q10. If the income of the family increases by 10% and the percentage distribution remains the same, what will be the new savings amount?
(A) Rs. 7520
(B) Rs. 7920
(C) Rs. 8320
(D) Rs. 8720
Answer: B
Explanation: 
New Income = 40000 * 1.10 = 44000.
Savings = 18% of 44000 = 0.18 * 44000 = 7920.

Directions (Q11-Q15): Study the given pie chart carefully and answer the following questions.
Refer to diagram: SVG/Q11.svg
The pie chart shows the percentage distribution of 1800 teachers who teach 5 different subjects (Math, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English).
(Math: 25%, Physics: 20%, Chemistry: 15%, Biology: 10%, English: 30%)

Q11. What is the total number of teachers teaching Physics and Chemistry together?
(A) 580
(B) 600
(C) 630
(D) 650
Answer: C
Explanation: 
Physics = 20%, Chemistry = 15%. Total = 35%.
Teachers = 35% of 1800 = 0.35 * 1800 = 630.

Q12. The number of English teachers is how much more than the number of Math teachers?
(A) 90
(B) 100
(C) 110
(D) 120
Answer: A
Explanation: 
English = 30%, Math = 25%.
Difference = 5%.
Teachers = 5% of 1800 = 90.

Q13. Find the ratio of the number of teachers teaching Biology to the number of teachers teaching Chemistry.
(A) 2 : 3
(B) 3 : 4
(C) 4 : 5
(D) 5 : 6
Answer: A
Explanation: 
Biology = 10%. Chemistry = 15%.
Ratio = 10 : 15 = 2 : 3.

Q14. The central angle for the sector representing Math teachers is:
(A) 75°
(B) 80°
(C) 90°
(D) 105°
Answer: C
Explanation: 
Math = 25%.
Central angle = 25% of 360° = (1/4) * 360° = 90°.

Q15. If 20% of the English teachers resign, what will be the new total number of teachers teaching in the school?
(A) 1680
(B) 1692
(C) 1708
(D) 1720
Answer: B
Explanation: 
English teachers = 30% of 1800 = 540.
Resigning = 20% of 540 = 108.
New total = 1800 - 108 = 1692.
