Directions (Q1-Q5): Study the given bar graph carefully and answer the following questions.
Refer to diagram: SVG/Q1.svg
The bar graph shows the production (in thousands) of two types of cars (Type A and Type B) by a company over 5 years (2018-2022).

Q1. What is the total production of Type A cars in the years 2019 and 2021 together?
(A) 85 thousand
(B) 90 thousand
(C) 95 thousand
(D) 100 thousand
Answer: B
Explanation: 
Production of Type A in 2019 = 40.
Production of Type A in 2021 = 50.
Total = 40 + 50 = 90 thousand.

Q2. The production of Type B cars in 2020 is what percent of the total production of both types of cars in 2018?
(A) 40%
(B) 45%
(C) 50%
(D) 55%
Answer: C
Explanation: 
Type B in 2020 = 35.
Total in 2018 = Type A (45) + Type B (25) = 70.
Percentage = (35 / 70) * 100 = 50%.

Q3. What is the ratio of the average production of Type A cars to the average production of Type B cars over the five years?
(A) 24 : 23
(B) 23 : 24
(C) 47 : 46
(D) 46 : 47
Answer: A
Explanation: 
Total Type A = 45(2018) + 40(2019) + 55(2020) + 50(2021) + 50(2022) = 240.
Total Type B = 25(2018) + 60(2019) + 35(2020) + 45(2021) + 65(2022) = 230.
Ratio of averages is the same as ratio of totals = 240 : 230 = 24 : 23.

Q5. If the total production of both types of cars in 2023 increases by 20% over that in 2022, what will be the total production in 2023?
(A) 128 thousand

Directions (Q6-Q10): Study the given horizontal bar graph carefully and answer the following questions.
Refer to diagram: SVG/Q6.svg
The bar graph shows the number of books sold by 5 different stores (S1 to S5) in two consecutive months, January and February.

Q6. What is the total number of books sold by all the stores in January?
(A) 1650
(B) 1700
(C) 1750
(D) 1800
Answer: C
Explanation: 
Sales in January: S1(300), S2(450), S3(250), S4(350), S5(400).
Total = 300 + 450 + 250 + 350 + 400 = 1750.

Q7. The number of books sold by Store S2 in February is what percent more than the number of books sold by Store S4 in January?
(A) 10%
(B) 15%
(C) 20%
(D) 25%
Answer: C
Explanation: 
S2 in Feb = 420.
S4 in Jan = 350.
Difference = 420 - 350 = 70.
% more = (70 / 350) * 100 = 20%.

Q8. Find the ratio of the total books sold by Store S1 to the total books sold by Store S5 in both months together.
(A) 33 : 43
(B) 43 : 53
(C) 11 : 16
(D) 33 : 38
Answer: C
Explanation: 
Total S1 = 300 (Jan) + 250 (Feb) = 550.
Total S5 = 400 (Jan) + 400 (Feb) = 800.
Ratio = 550 : 800 = 55 : 80 = 11 : 16.

Q9. Which store had the maximum percentage decrease in sales from January to February?
(A) S1
(B) S2
(C) S3
(D) S4
Answer: A
Explanation: 
Decreases:
S1: from 300 to 250 -> Decrease of 50. % dec = (50/300)*100 = 16.66%.
S2: from 450 to 420 -> Decrease of 30. % dec = (30/450)*100 = 6.66%.
S3: from 250 to 320 -> Increase.
S4: from 350 to 380 -> Increase.
S5: from 400 to 400 -> No change.
Store S1 had the maximum percentage decrease.

Q10. What is the average number of books sold by Store S3 and Store S4 together over both months?
(A) 625
(B) 650
(C) 675
(D) 700
Answer: B
Explanation: 
Total S3 = 250 + 320 = 570.
Total S4 = 350 + 380 = 730.
Sum of both stores = 570 + 730 = 1300.
Since we want the average per store (for the 2 stores total), it is 1300 / 2 = 650.

Directions (Q11-Q15): Study the given stacked bar graph carefully and answer the following questions.
Refer to diagram: SVG/Q11.svg
The stacked bar graph shows the number of Valid and Invalid votes cast in 5 different villages (V1, V2, V3, V4, V5) during an election.

Q11. What is the total number of valid votes cast across all 5 villages?
(A) 10500
(B) 11000
(C) 11500
(D) 12000
Answer: C
Explanation: 
Valid votes: V1(2000), V2(2500), V3(1800), V4(3000), V5(2200).
Total = 2000 + 2500 + 1800 + 3000 + 2200 = 11500.

Q12. In which village is the percentage of invalid votes out of the total votes cast the maximum?
(A) V1
(B) V2
(C) V3
(D) V5
Answer: C
Explanation: 
Invalid %:
V1: Invalid=500, Total=2500 -> 500/2500 = 20%.
V2: Invalid=500, Total=3000 -> 500/3000 = 16.66%.
V3: Invalid=600, Total=2400 -> 600/2400 = 25%.
V4: Invalid=600, Total=3600 -> 600/3600 = 16.66%.
V5: Invalid=300, Total=2500 -> 300/2500 = 12%.
Village V3 has the maximum percentage (25%).

Q13. The total number of invalid votes in V1 and V2 together is what percent of the valid votes in V4?
(A) 25%
(B) 33.33%
(C) 40%
(D) 50%
Answer: B
Explanation: 
Invalid in V1 + V2 = 500 + 500 = 1000.
Valid in V4 = 3000.
Percentage = (1000 / 3000) * 100 = 33.33%.

Q14. Find the ratio of total votes cast in Village V2 to the total votes cast in Village V5.
(A) 5 : 4
(B) 6 : 5
(C) 7 : 6
(D) 8 : 7
Answer: B
Explanation: 
Total votes in V2 = 2500(Valid) + 500(Invalid) = 3000.
Total votes in V5 = 2200(Valid) + 300(Invalid) = 2500.
Ratio = 3000 : 2500 = 6 : 5.

Q15. If the winning candidate in Village V4 secured 55% of the valid votes, how many valid votes did the losing candidates collectively secure in V4?
(A) 1250
(B) 1300
(C) 1350
(D) 1400
Answer: C
Explanation: 
Total valid votes in V4 = 3000.
Winning candidate got 55%.
Losing candidates got (100% - 55%) = 45% of valid votes.
45% of 3000 = 0.45 * 3000 = 1350.
