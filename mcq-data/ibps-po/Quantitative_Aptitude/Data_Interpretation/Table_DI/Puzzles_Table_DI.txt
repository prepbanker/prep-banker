The table shows the sales (in thousands) of four different products (P, Q, R, S) over five years (2018-2022).

Q1. What is the average sales of Product Q over all the given years?
(A) 44.4 thousand
(B) 45.6 thousand
(C) 46.2 thousand
(D) 48.0 thousand
Answer: B
Explanation: 
Sales of Q: 42 (2018) + 48 (2019) + 45 (2020) + 50 (2021) + 43 (2022) = 228.
Average = 228 / 5 = 45.6 thousand.

Q2. The total sales of Product R in 2019 and 2020 together is approximately what percent of the total sales of Product S in 2021 and 2022 together?
(A) 82%
(B) 85%
(C) 88%
(D) 91%
Answer: C
Explanation: 
Sales of R in 2019 and 2020 = 55 + 50 = 105.
Sales of S in 2021 and 2022 = 62 + 58 = 120.
Percentage = (105 / 120) * 100 = (7/8) * 100 = 87.5% ≈ 88%.

Q3. What is the ratio of the total sales of all products in 2018 to that in 2022?
(A) 165 : 182
(B) 175 : 191
(C) 182 : 199
(D) 191 : 205
Answer: C
Explanation: 
Total sales in 2018 = 35 (P) + 42 (Q) + 60 (R) + 45 (S) = 182.
Total sales in 2022 = 48 (P) + 43 (Q) + 50 (R) + 58 (S) = 199.
Ratio = 182 : 199.

Q4. If the sales of Product P in 2023 is expected to increase by 20% compared to 2022, what will be the sales of Product P in 2023 (in thousands)?
(A) 55.6
(B) 57.6
(C) 59.6
(D) 61.6
Answer: B
Explanation: 
Sales of P in 2022 = 48.
Expected increase = 20% of 48 = 9.6.
Sales in 2023 = 48 + 9.6 = 57.6 thousand.

Q5. Which product showed the highest percentage increase in sales from 2018 to 2021?
(A) P
(B) Q
(C) R
(D) S
Answer: D
Explanation: 
P: from 35 to 45 -> Increase = 10 -> (10/35)*100 = 28.57%
Q: from 42 to 50 -> Increase = 8 -> (8/42)*100 = 19.04%
R: from 60 to 52 -> Decrease.
S: from 45 to 62 -> Increase = 17 -> (17/45)*100 = 37.77%
Product S showed the highest percentage increase.
