Q1. What will come in place of question mark (?) in the following question?
120 / 4 + 15 * 3 - ? = 40
(A) 35
(B) 40
(C) 45
(D) 50
Answer: A
Explanation: 
Using BODMAS rule:
(120 / 4) + (15 * 3) - ? = 40
30 + 45 - ? = 40
75 - ? = 40
? = 75 - 40 = 35.

Q2. What is the value of 18 + 12 / 3 * 2 - 4?
(A) 22
(B) 16
(C) 20
(D) 24
Answer: A
Explanation: 
Using BODMAS rule:
Division first: 12 / 3 = 4
Multiplication next: 4 * 2 = 8
Then addition and subtraction: 18 + 8 - 4
26 - 4 = 22.

Q4. Solve for (?): 45 - [38 - {60 / 3 - (6 - 9 / 3)}] = ?
(A) 22
(B) 24
(C) 26
(D) 28
Answer: B
Explanation: 
Solve inner bracket first: (6 - 9 / 3) = 6 - 3 = 3.
Curly bracket: {60 / 3 - 3} = {20 - 3} = 17.
Square bracket: [38 - 17] = 21.
Final: 45 - 21 = 24.
