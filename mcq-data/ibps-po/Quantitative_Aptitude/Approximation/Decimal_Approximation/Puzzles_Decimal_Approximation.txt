Topic: Approximation
Subtopic: Decimal Approximation

Q1. What approximate value should come in place of question mark (?) in the following question?
12.05 * 5.98 + 15.11 = ?
(A) 82
(B) 87
(C) 92
(D) 97
Answer: B
Explanation: 
Approximating the values:
12.05 ≈ 12
5.98 ≈ 6
15.11 ≈ 15
Equation becomes: 12 * 6 + 15 = 72 + 15 = 87.

Q2. Find the approximate value: 45.02 / 8.99 * 11.95 = ?

(B) 55
(C) 60
(D) 65
Answer: C
Explanation: 
Approximating:
45.02 ≈ 45
8.99 ≈ 9

Equation: (45 / 9) * 12 = 5 * 12 = 60.

Q15. What approximate value will come in place of (?): (31.98)^2 - (24.01)^2 + 149.95 = ?
(A) 578
(B) 588
(C) 598
(D) 608
Answer: C
Explanation: 
Approximating:
31.98 ≈ 32
24.01 ≈ 24
149.95 ≈ 150
Equation: 32^2 - 24^2 + 150
Using a^2 - b^2 = (a-b)(a+b):
(32-24)(32+24) + 150 = 8 * 56 + 150 = 448 + 150 = 598.
