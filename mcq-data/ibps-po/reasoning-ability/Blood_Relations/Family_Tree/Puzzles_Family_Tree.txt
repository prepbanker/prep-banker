Q1. A is the father of B. C is the brother of A. F is the sister of B. If M is the father of A, then establish a relationship between F and C.
(A) F is the daughter of C
(B) F is the niece of C
(C) F is the sister of C
(D) F is the cousin of C
Answer: B
Explanation: 
A is the father of B and F (since F is B's sister). C is the brother of A. Therefore, C is the uncle of F. Thus, F is the niece of C.

Q2. Pointing to a photograph of a boy, Suresh said, "He is the son of the only son of my mother." How is Suresh related to that boy?
(A) Brother
(B) Uncle
(C) Cousin
(D) Father
Answer: D
Explanation: 
"The only son of my mother" is Suresh himself. Therefore, the boy is the son of Suresh. Hence, Suresh is the father of the boy.

Q3. If A is the brother of B; B is the sister of C; and C is the father of D, how D is related to A?
(A) Brother
(B) Sister
(C) Nephew
(D) Cannot be determined
Answer: D
Explanation: 
A is the brother of B. B is the sister of C. C is the father of D. This means A, B, and C are siblings, and D is the child of C. Therefore, A is the uncle of D. However, the gender of D is not given, so D could be a nephew or a niece. The exact relationship cannot be determined.

Q4. Pointing to a woman, a man says, "Her mother is the only daughter of my mother-in-law." How is the man related to the woman?
(A) Father
(B) Husband
(C) Brother
(D) Uncle
Answer: A
Explanation: 
"Only daughter of my mother-in-law" is the man's wife. Therefore, the woman's mother is the man's wife. This means the man is the father of the woman.

Q5. A woman introduces a man as the son of the brother of her mother. How is the man related to the woman?
(A) Nephew
(B) Son
(C) Cousin
(D) Uncle
Answer: C
Explanation: 
"Brother of her mother" is the woman's uncle (maternal uncle). The son of her uncle is her cousin. Therefore, the man is the woman's cousin.

Q6. In a family of six persons A, B, C, D, E, and F, there are two married couples. D is the grandmother of A and mother of B. C is the wife of B and mother of F. F is the granddaughter of E. What is the relation of A to F?
(A) Brother
(B) Sister
(C) Cousin
(D) Cannot be determined
Answer: D
Explanation: 
D is the mother of B. C is the wife of B (so B is a male, C is a female). D is the grandmother of A. C is the mother of F. F is the granddaughter of E. Since there are two married couples and D is a grandmother, D must be married to E (E is the grandfather). A and F are children of B and C. So A and F are siblings. However, the gender of A is not given. Therefore, A could be a brother or a sister to F. Cannot be determined.

Q9. Q's mother is the sister of P and daughter of M. S is the daughter of P and sister of T. How is M related to T?
(A) Grandmother
(B) Father
(C) Grandfather
(D) Either Grandmother or Grandfather
Answer: D
Explanation: 
Q's mother (let's call her X) is the sister of P and daughter of M. So, X and P are siblings, and M is their parent. S is the daughter of P and sister of T. So, S and T are children of P. Since P is the child of M, T is the grandchild of M. Therefore, M is the grandparent of T. Since M's gender is unknown, M is either Grandmother or Grandfather.

Q10. A and B are young ones of C. If C is the father of A but B is not the son of C. How are B and C related?
(A) Niece and Uncle
(B) Daughter and Father
(C) Nephew and Uncle
(D) Daughter and Mother
Answer: B
Explanation: 
A and B are children of C. C is the father of A (and thus the father of B). If B is not the son of C, then B must be the daughter of C. Therefore, the relationship is Daughter and Father.

Q14. A family consists of six members M, N, O, P, Q, and R. There are two married couples. M is a doctor and the father of P. U is not a member, let's use Q. Q is a teacher and the mother of R. N is the grandmother of P and is a housewife. O is the brother of M. R is the sister of P. How is O related to R?
(A) Brother
(B) Uncle
(C) Father
(D) Grandfather
Answer: B
Explanation: 
M is the father of P. Q is the mother of R. R is the sister of P. This means P and R are siblings, and their parents are M (father) and Q (mother). So M and Q are a married couple. N is the grandmother of P. So N is the mother of M (since M is the father). O is the brother of M. Since O is M's brother, O is the uncle of M's children (P and R). Therefore, O is the uncle of R.

Q15. In a family of three generations, there are eight members: J, K, L, M, N, O, P, and Q. J is the father of M. N is the only daughter of K. M is married to N. O is the brother of N. P is the son of M. Q is the sister of P. L is the mother of M. How is L related to Q?
(Refer to diagram: SVG/Q15.svg)
(A) Grandmother
(B) Mother
(C) Aunt
(D) Sister
Answer: A
Explanation: 
J is the father of M, and L is the mother of M. Therefore, J and L are married, and M is their son (since N is the only daughter of K, and M is married to N, M must be a male). M and N are married. P is the son of M, and Q is the sister of P. Therefore, P and Q are the children of M and N. L is the mother of M, which means L is the paternal grandmother of M's children (P and Q). Thus, L is the grandmother of Q.
