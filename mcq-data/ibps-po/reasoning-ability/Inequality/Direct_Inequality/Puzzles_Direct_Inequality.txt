Topic: Inequality
Subtopic: Direct Inequality

Q1. Statements: A > B ≥ C = D < E
Conclusions:
I. A > D
II. B < E
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Either I or II follows
(D) Both I and II follow
Answer: A
Explanation: 
From the statement, we have A > B ≥ C = D. Connecting these, A > D. Hence, conclusion I is true. For conclusion II, we look at B ≥ C = D < E. Since there are opposite signs (≥ and <) between B and E, no definite relation can be established. Hence, conclusion II is false. Only conclusion I follows.
