Topic: Syllogism
Subtopic: Basic Syllogism

Q1. Statements:
Some cats are dogs.
All dogs are birds.
Conclusions:
I. Some cats are birds.
II. All cats are birds.
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Either I or II follows
(D) Both I and II follow
Answer: A
Explanation: 
From the statements, the area of 'dogs' is entirely inside 'birds'. Since 'some cats' are 'dogs', that overlapping part must also be inside 'birds'. Therefore, 'Some cats are birds' is definitely true. There is no information to suggest that 'All cats are birds', so conclusion II does not follow. Thus, only conclusion I follows.

Q2. Statements:
All pens are pencils.
No pencil is an eraser.
Conclusions:
I. No pen is an eraser.
II. Some pens are erasers.
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Neither I nor II follows
(D) Both I and II follow
Answer: A
Explanation: 
'All pens are pencils' means the circle of pens is completely within the circle of pencils. 'No pencil is an eraser' means there is no intersection between pencils and erasers. Since pens are entirely inside pencils, they can never intersect with erasers. Thus, 'No pen is an eraser' is true, making I true and II false.

Q3. Statements:
Some books are tables.
Some tables are chairs.
Conclusions:
I. Some books are chairs.
II. No book is a chair.
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Either I or II follows
(D) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: C
Explanation: 
The statements show a possible relationship where books and tables intersect, and tables and chairs intersect. There is no direct connection given between books and chairs. Thus, we cannot definitely say 'Some books are chairs' or 'No book is a chair'. However, these two form a complementary pair (Some + No). Therefore, either some books are chairs or no book is a chair. Either I or II follows.

Q4. Statements:
All cars are buses.
All buses are trains.
Conclusions:
I. All cars are trains.
II. Some trains are cars.
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Neither I nor II follows
(D) Both I and II follow
Answer: D
Explanation: 
The circle for cars is inside buses, and buses are inside trains. This makes cars completely inside trains. So, 'All cars are trains' is true. Since the cars circle is a part of the trains circle, 'Some trains are cars' is also true. Thus, both conclusions follow.

Q5. Statements:
No red is blue.
Some blues are greens.
Conclusions:
I. Some greens are not red.
II. Some red are not greens.
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Either I or II follows
(D) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: A
Explanation: 
We know no red is blue. And some blues are greens. The part of greens which is blue can never be red. Therefore, 'Some greens are not red' is definitely true. However, we don't have enough information to say 'Some red are not greens', as red could potentially be entirely within the non-blue part of greens. So only conclusion I follows.

Q6. Statements:
All laptops are computers.
Some computers are tablets.
No tablet is a mobile.
Conclusions:
I. Some laptops are tablets.
II. No laptop is a mobile.
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Either I or II follows
(D) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: D
Explanation: 
We know laptops are inside computers, and computers intersect with tablets. However, the intersection of computers and tablets does not necessarily include laptops. Thus, 'Some laptops are tablets' is not definite. Similarly, while no tablet is a mobile, there is no direct negative relation between laptops and mobiles. So 'No laptop is a mobile' is not definite. Neither conclusion follows.

Q7. Statements:
Some clouds are rains.
All rains are waters.
No water is a river.
Conclusions:
I. Some clouds are not rivers.
II. No rain is a river.
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Both I and II follow
(D) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: C
Explanation: 
'All rains are waters' and 'No water is a river' means 'No rain is a river' is definitely true (Conclusion II follows). 'Some clouds are rains', and since all rains are waters (which cannot be rivers), the part of clouds that is rain can never be a river. Therefore, 'Some clouds are not rivers' is definitely true (Conclusion I follows). Both I and II follow.

Q13. Statements:
All A are B.
All B are C.
No C is D.
Some D are E.
Conclusions:
I. No A is D.
II. Some E are not B.
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Both I and II follow
(D) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: C
Explanation: 
Since all A are inside B, and all B are inside C, A is entirely inside C. Since no C is D, it follows that no A is D. Conclusion I is true. We know some D are E, and no D is C (and consequently no D is B). The part of E that is D can never be B. Thus, conclusion II is true. Both conclusions follow.

Q15. Statements:
No manager is a leader.
Some leaders are executives.
All executives are directors.
No director is a clerk.
Conclusions:
I. Some directors are not managers.
II. Some executives are not clerks.
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Both I and II follow
(D) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: C
Explanation: 
'Some leaders are executives' and 'All executives are directors' implies some leaders are directors. Since 'No manager is a leader', the directors that are leaders can never be managers. Thus, 'Some directors are not managers' is true. 'All executives are directors' and 'No director is a clerk' implies 'No executive is a clerk'. Therefore, 'Some executives are not clerks' is also logically true (if none are, then some are not is true). Both conclusions follow.
