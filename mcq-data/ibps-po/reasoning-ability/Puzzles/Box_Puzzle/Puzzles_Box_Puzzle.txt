Directions (Q1-Q5): Study the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
Seven boxes – A, B, C, D, E, F, and G are placed one above another in a stack. The bottommost position is numbered 1 and the topmost position is numbered 7.
- Box C is placed immediately above box A.
- Three boxes are placed between C and F.
- Box E is placed immediately below box F.
- Box D is placed at the topmost position.
- Box B is placed somewhere above box G but below box A.

Q1. Which box is kept at the bottommost position?
(A) A
(B) E
(C) F
(D) G

Answer: B
Explanation:
Step 1: D is at the topmost position (7).
Step 2: C is placed immediately above A.
Step 3: Three boxes are placed between C and F. If C is at 6, F will be at 2.
Step 4: E is placed immediately below F. So, E is at 1.
Step 5: B is above G but below A. The remaining positions are 5, 4, 3. Since C is 6, A is 5. Therefore, B is 4 and G is 3.
Final Arrangement:
7 - D
6 - C
5 - A
4 - B
3 - G
2 - F
1 - E
Thus, E is kept at the bottommost position.

Q2. How many boxes are kept between D and B?
(A) One
(B) Two
(C) Three
(D) None

Answer: B
Explanation:
Boxes C and A are kept between D (7th) and B (4th). Thus, exactly two boxes are between them.

Q3. Which of the following box is kept immediately above G?
(A) B
(B) A
(C) F
(D) C

Answer: A
Explanation:
Box B is placed at the 4th position, which is immediately above Box G at the 3rd position.

Q4. Three of the following four are alike in a certain way and hence form a group. Which of the following does not belong to the group?
(A) D - A
(B) C - B
(C) A - G
(D) F - C

Answer: D
Explanation:
In options A, B, and C, there is exactly one box between the two given boxes (D and A have C; C and B have A; A and G have B). However, between F and C, there are three boxes. Thus, F - C is the odd one out.

Q5. If all boxes are arranged alphabetically from top to bottom, whose position will remain unchanged?
(A) A
(B) D
(C) G
(D) None

Answer: D
Explanation:
Original (Top to Bottom): D, C, A, B, G, F, E.
Alphabetical (Top to Bottom): A, B, C, D, E, F, G.
None of the boxes retain their original positions.

Directions (Q6-Q10): Study the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
Eight boxes P, Q, R, S, T, U, V, and W are placed one above another. They contain different items: Pen, Pencil, Eraser, Scale, Book, Copy, Marker, and Chalk.
- Box U contains Chalk and is placed at the bottommost position.
- Three boxes are placed between U and T, which contains Pencil.
- Box R contains Scale and is placed immediately below T.
- Box V contains Book and is placed at the topmost position.
- Only one box is placed between V and Q, and both are placed above T.
- Box S contains Eraser and is placed immediately above Q.
- Box P contains Marker and is placed immediately above W.
- Box Q contains Pen.

Q6. Which box is placed immediately below the box containing Marker?
(A) P
(B) W
(C) U
(D) R

Answer: B
Explanation:
Step 1: U contains Chalk and is at the bottom (1st).
Step 2: Three boxes between U and T. So, T is at 5th position and contains Pencil.
Step 3: R contains Scale and is immediately below T (4th position).
Step 4: V contains Book and is at the topmost (8th position).
Step 5: One box is between V and Q (both above T). V is 8, so Q is 6. Q contains Pen.
Step 6: S contains Eraser and is immediately above Q. So, S is at 7th position.
Step 7: P contains Marker and is immediately above W. The remaining consecutive positions are 3 and 2. So, P is 3rd and W is 2nd. W must contain the remaining item, Copy.
Final Arrangement:
8 - V - Book
7 - S - Eraser
6 - Q - Pen
5 - T - Pencil
4 - R - Scale
3 - P - Marker
2 - W - Copy
1 - U - Chalk
Therefore, W is placed immediately below P (Marker).

(A) Marker
(B) Copy

(D) Scale

Answer: B
Explanation:
As per the final arrangement, Box W contains Copy.

Q10. Three of the following four are alike in a certain way and hence form a group. Which of the following does not belong to the group?
(A) V - Eraser
(B) S - Pen

Directions (Q11-Q15): Study the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
Eight boxes – A, B, C, D, E, F, G, and H are placed in a stack of 9 positions where one position is empty. Each box has a different weight (10kg, 20kg, 30kg, 40kg, 50kg, 60kg, 70kg, and 80kg).
- The topmost position contains a box that weighs 30kg.
- There are two positions between the box weighing 30kg and box D.
- The empty position is an odd-numbered position above D.
- The box immediately above the empty position weighs 80kg.
- Three positions are between the empty position and box B.
- F is placed immediately below H.
- H is placed immediately below B.
- Box E is placed somewhere between D and B.
- Box A is placed immediately above B.
- Box B weighs one-fourth of Box G.
- Box A weighs 20kg more than B.
- The total weight of boxes F and H is 70kg, and H is heavier than F.
- Box D weighs 50kg.
- Box E weighs more than H.

Q11. Which position is empty?
(A) 3rd
(B) 5th
(C) 7th
(D) 9th

Answer: C
Explanation:
Step 1: Topmost position (9th) has 30kg box.
Step 2: Two positions between 9th and D means D is at 6th position. D = 50kg.
Step 3: Empty position is odd and above D. Options are 7 or 9. Since 9 is occupied, 7th position is empty.
Step 4: Three positions between empty (7) and B means B is at 3rd position.
Step 5: H immediately below B (2nd position), F immediately below H (1st position).
Step 6: A immediately above B (4th position).
Step 7: E is between D (6) and B (3). The only available position is 5th. So E is 5th.
Step 8: The remaining box G must be at 8th position. The topmost box is C (30kg).
Step 9: G is immediately above empty (7), so G = 80kg.
Step 10: B is 1/4 of G, so B = 20kg.
Step 11: A is 20kg more than B, so A = 40kg.
Step 12: F + H = 70kg, H > F. The remaining weights are 10, 60, 70. So H = 60kg, F = 10kg.
Step 13: E must weigh the remaining 70kg (which is > H(60kg)).
Final Arrangement:
9 - C - 30kg
8 - G - 80kg
7 - Empty
6 - D - 50kg
5 - E - 70kg
4 - A - 40kg
3 - B - 20kg
2 - H - 60kg
1 - F - 10kg
Therefore, the 7th position is empty.

Q15. Three of the following four are alike in a certain way and hence form a group. Which of the following does not belong to the group?
(A) C - 80kg
(B) D - 70kg
(C) A - 20kg
(D) E - 60kg

Answer: D
Explanation:
In options A, B, and C, the weight corresponds to the box placed immediately below the given box.
C (9) - 80kg (8)
D (6) - 70kg (5)
A (4) - 20kg (3)
For E (5) - 60kg (2), there is a gap of two boxes. Hence, it does not belong to the group.
