Directions (Q1-Q5): Study the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
Seven persons – A, B, C, D, E, F, and G live on seven different floors of a building but not necessarily in the same order. The lowermost floor of the building is numbered 1, the one above that is numbered 2, and so on till the topmost floor is numbered 7.
- A lives on an even-numbered floor but not on floor 2.
- Only two persons live between A and B.
- C lives immediately above B.
- D lives on an odd-numbered floor above A.
- E lives immediately below F.
- G does not live on the ground floor.
- F does not live on an even-numbered floor.

Q1. Who lives on the topmost floor?
(A) A
(B) D
(C) F
(D) E

Answer: C
Explanation:
Step 1: A lives on an even-numbered floor but not on floor 2. So, A can be on floor 4 or 6.
Step 2: Two persons live between A and B.
Case 1: A is on 4. Then B is on 1 or 7.
Case 2: A is on 6. Then B is on 3.
Step 3: C lives immediately above B. In Case 1, if B is 7, C would be 8 (not possible). So B is 1, and C is 2. In Case 2, B is 3, and C is 4.
Step 4: D lives on an odd-numbered floor above A. In Case 1 (A=4), D can be on 5 or 7. In Case 2 (A=6), D must be on 7.
Step 5: E lives immediately below F, and F does not live on an even-numbered floor. This means F must be on an odd floor, and E on an even floor directly below.
In Case 1: If D=5, the contiguous empty floors are 6 and 7. F(odd) goes to 7, E(even) goes to 6. Valid! Floor 3 is left for G. G is not on the ground floor, which satisfies the condition.
In Case 2: A=6, C=4, B=3, D=7. Empty floors are 1, 2, and 5. Contiguous empty floors are 1 and 2. F(odd) goes to 2 (invalid, F must not be even). Case 2 is eliminated.
Final Arrangement:
7 - F
6 - E
5 - D
4 - A
3 - G
2 - C
1 - B
Therefore, F lives on the topmost floor.

Q2. How many persons live between D and C?
(A) One
(B) Two
(C) Three
(D) None

Answer: B
Explanation:
Based on the final arrangement, D lives on floor 5 and C lives on floor 2.
The persons living between them are A (floor 4) and G (floor 3).
Thus, two persons live between D and C.

According to the final arrangement derived, G lives on Floor 3.

Directions (Q6-Q10): Study the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
Eight persons – P, Q, R, S, T, U, V, and W live on an 8-story building. The ground floor is numbered 1, and the topmost floor is numbered 8. Each of them likes a different color: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Black, White, Pink, and Orange.
- V lives on an odd-numbered floor above floor 4 and likes Pink.
- Three persons live between V and T, who likes White.
- Q likes Blue and lives immediately above P.
- P lives immediately above T.
- The one who likes Black lives immediately below V.
- S lives on an even-numbered floor above Q.
- W lives on the topmost floor and likes Orange.
- R lives immediately above the one who likes Green.
- P does not like Red.
- The one who likes Red lives on an even-numbered floor.

Q6. Who among the following likes Black?
(A) S
(B) Q
(C) R
(D) W

Answer: A
Explanation:
Step 1: V lives on an odd floor above 4 (so 5 or 7).
Step 2: If V=5, three persons between V and T means T=1 (White). P lives immediately above T, so P=2. Q lives immediately above P, so Q=3 (Blue). The one who likes Black is immediately below V, so Black=4. S lives on an even floor above Q, so S=4, 6, or 8. W is on floor 8 (Orange), so S=4 or 6. R lives immediately above the one who likes Green. If Green=7 (only odd left), then R=8 (Not possible, W is 8). Thus V=5 is invalid.
Step 3: If V=7 (Pink), three persons between V and T means T=3 (White). P is above T (P=4), Q is above P (Q=5, Blue). Black is below V (Black=6). S is on an even floor above Q, so S=6 or 8. Since W=8 (Orange), S=6 (Black).
Step 4: R lives immediately above Green (odd floor). Remaining odd floors are 1 (since 3, 5, 7 are taken). So Green=1, R=2. The only remaining person U must be on floor 1 (Green).
Step 5: The one who likes Red lives on an even floor. P(4) and R(2) are even. P does not like Red, so P likes Yellow, and R likes Red.
Final Arrangement:
8 - W - Orange
7 - V - Pink
6 - S - Black
5 - Q - Blue
4 - P - Yellow
3 - T - White
2 - R - Red
1 - U - Green
Therefore, S likes Black.

Directions (Q11-Q15): Study the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
Ten persons – A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, and J live in a 5-story building. The lowermost floor is numbered 1 and the topmost floor is numbered 5. Each floor has two flats – Flat P and Flat Q. Flat P is to the west of Flat Q.
- D lives on an odd-numbered floor in Flat Q.
- Only one floor is between D and E, and both live in the same flat.
- A lives immediately above E but in a different flat.
- There are two floors between A and F, and both live in the same flat.
- C lives to the east of F.
- H lives immediately above B in the same flat.
- B lives on an even-numbered floor.
- G lives above I in the same flat.
- J lives to the north-west of G.

Q11. Who lives in Flat P of Floor 3?
(A) B
(B) A
(C) H
(D) J

Answer: C
Explanation:
Step 1: D is on an odd floor in Flat Q (1Q, 3Q, or 5Q).
Step 2: Let's test D = 5Q. One floor between D and E (same flat) gives E = 3Q.
Step 3: A lives immediately above E in a different flat, so A = 4P.
Step 4: Two floors between A and F (same flat) means F = 1P.
Step 5: C lives to the east of F, meaning C = 1Q.
Step 6: B lives on an even floor. Options are 2 or 4. H is immediately above B. Since 4P has A, B cannot be 4P. If B=4Q, H=5Q (but 5Q is D). So B must be on Floor 2. If B=2Q, H=3Q (but 3Q is E). So B = 2P and H = 3P.
Step 7: J lives north-west of G, meaning J is in Flat P and G is in Flat Q. J is on floor 5P.
Step 8: G lives above I in the same flat (Flat Q). Remaining slots in Flat Q are 4Q and 2Q, so G = 4Q and I = 2Q.
This satisfies all conditions perfectly. H lives in Flat P of Floor 3.

Hence, A and G live on the same floor (Floor 4).
