Directions (Q1-Q5): Study the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
Seven friends – M, N, O, P, Q, R, and S attend a seminar on seven different days of the same week starting from Monday to Sunday.
- O attends the seminar on Thursday.
- Two persons attend between O and P.
- M attends immediately before P.
- Three persons attend between M and N.
- R attends before S but after Q.

Q1. Who attends the seminar on Monday?
(A) P
(B) Q
(C) R
(D) S

Answer: B
Explanation:
Step 1: O attends on Thursday.
Step 2: Two persons between O and P means P can attend on Monday or Sunday.
Step 3: M attends immediately before P. If P is Monday, M would be Sunday (previous week, invalid). So P attends on Sunday and M attends on Saturday.
Step 4: Three persons between M (Saturday) and N means N attends on Tuesday.
Step 5: R attends before S but after Q. The remaining days are Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. Thus, Q is on Monday, R on Wednesday, and S on Friday.
Final Arrangement:
Monday - Q
Tuesday - N
Wednesday - R
Thursday - O
Friday - S
Saturday - M
Sunday - P
Therefore, Q attends on Monday.

Q2. How many persons attend between N and S?
(A) One
(B) Two
(C) Three
(D) None

Answer: B
Explanation:
N attends on Tuesday and S attends on Friday.
R and O attend between them on Wednesday and Thursday. Thus, two persons attend between N and S.

Q3. On which day does R attend the seminar?
(A) Tuesday
(B) Wednesday
(C) Thursday
(D) Friday

Answer: B
Explanation:
According to the final arrangement, R attends on Wednesday.

Q4. Who attends immediately after M?
(A) N
(B) O
(C) P
(D) Q

Answer: C
Explanation:
M attends on Saturday and P attends immediately after on Sunday.

Q5. Three of the following four are alike in a certain way and hence form a group. Which of the following does not belong to the group?
(A) Q - Wednesday
(B) N - Thursday
(C) O - Saturday
(D) R - Saturday

Answer: D
Explanation:
Q (Monday) and Wednesday have a gap of one day.
N (Tuesday) and Thursday have a gap of one day.
O (Thursday) and Saturday have a gap of one day.
R (Wednesday) and Saturday have a gap of two days. Thus, R - Saturday is the odd one out.

Directions (Q6-Q10): Study the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
Seven people – A, B, C, D, E, F, and G travel to different cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Pune, Goa, Agra, Patna, and Kochi) on seven different days of the same week from Monday to Sunday.
- C goes to Pune on Thursday.
- The one who goes to Mumbai goes immediately before F, who goes to Goa.
- Three persons go between F and A, who goes to Delhi.
- B goes to Patna immediately after D, who goes to Agra.
- G does not go to Mumbai.

Q6. Who goes on Friday?
(A) A
(B) E
(C) G
(D) F

Answer: C
Explanation:
Step 1: C goes to Pune on Thursday.
Step 2: F goes to Goa. The one who goes to Mumbai goes immediately before F. If F is on Sunday, Mumbai is Saturday. Three persons between F(Sunday) and A means A goes on Wednesday (Delhi).
Step 3: D goes to Agra and B goes to Patna immediately after D. The only consecutive days left are Monday and Tuesday. So D is Monday and B is Tuesday.
Step 4: The remaining days are Friday and Saturday. The remaining persons are E and G. G does not go to Mumbai, so G goes on Friday and E goes on Saturday (Mumbai).
Step 5: The only city left is Kochi, which goes to G.
Final Arrangement:
Monday - D - Agra
Tuesday - B - Patna
Wednesday - A - Delhi
Thursday - C - Pune
Friday - G - Kochi
Saturday - E - Mumbai
Sunday - F - Goa
Therefore, G goes on Friday.

(B) A - Pune

D (Mon) - Patna (Tue)
A (Wed) - Pune (Thu)
G (Fri) - Mumbai (Sat)
However, for option D, E (Sat) and Kochi (Fri), the city is of the previous day. Hence, D is the odd one out.

- S attends on Wednesday and likes Yellow.
- Three persons attend between S and T.
- The one who likes Black works in IBM.
- R likes Green, works in Wipro, and attends on Saturday.
- Q works in Infosys and attends immediately before R.
- The one who likes White attends immediately before the one who likes Red.
- P works in TCS and likes Red.
- The one who works in TechM attends on Monday.
- V attends immediately after S and likes Pink.
- V works in Cognizant.

Q11. Who attends the meeting on Monday?
(A) P
(B) U
(C) T
(D) S

Answer: B
Explanation:
Step 1: S is on Wednesday (Yellow).
Step 2: Three persons between S and T means T is on Sunday.
Step 3: V attends immediately after S (Thursday) and likes Pink, works in Cognizant.
Step 4: R is on Saturday (Green, Wipro). Q attends immediately before R, so Q is on Friday (Infosys).
Step 5: The one who works in TechM is on Monday.
Step 6: White is immediately before Red. The only free consecutive days are Monday and Tuesday. So Monday is White and Tuesday is Red.
Step 7: P works in TCS and likes Red (Tuesday).
Step 8: The remaining person U must be on Monday (TechM, White).
Step 9: T is on Sunday. Black works in IBM, so T likes Black and works in IBM.
Step 10: S (Wednesday) is left with the company HCL.
Final Arrangement:
Monday - U - TechM - White
Tuesday - P - TCS - Red
Wednesday - S - HCL - Yellow
Thursday - V - Cognizant - Pink
Friday - Q - Infosys - Blue (Blue is the remaining color)
Saturday - R - Wipro - Green
Sunday - T - IBM - Black
Therefore, U attends on Monday.
