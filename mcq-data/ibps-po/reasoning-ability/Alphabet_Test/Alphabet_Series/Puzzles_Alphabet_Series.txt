Q1. What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following series?
A, C, F, J, ?
(A) M
(B) N
(C) O
(D) P
Answer: C
Explanation: 
A(+2) = C. C(+3) = F. F(+4) = J. J(+5) = O. The missing letter is O.

Q10. Which group of letters should come in the blank? _ b a a _ b b _ a b _ a
(A) b a a b
(B) b a b a
(C) a a b b
(D) a b a b
Answer: A
Explanation: 
Let's try breaking the sequence into blocks of 3 or 4.
_ b a / a _ b / b _ a / b _ a (Not working).
Let's try block of 4: _ b a a / _ b b _ / a b _ a
If we fill with option A: (b) b a a / (a) b b (a) / a b (b) a. No clear pattern.
Let's try another logic. What if the pattern is 'a b a a b b'?
b b a a / a b b a / a b b a
Let's fill option C (a a b b):
(a) b a a / (a) b b (b) / a b (b) a. -> a b a a a b b b a b b a. (No).
Let's find the repeating unit.
_ b a a _ b b _ a b _ a
Notice 'b a a', 'b b', 'a b _ a'.
What if the pattern is 'a b a / a b b / a b a / a b b'?
Fill with a, b, a, b.
Sequence: (a) b a / a (b) b / (a) a b / (b) a. No.
What if it is 'b b a a b b a a...'?
(b) b a a / (b) b b (a) -> no.
What if it is 'a b a a b a a b a a b a'?
(a) b a a / (b) b a a / (a) b a a? No, 2nd block is _ b b _.
The standard pattern could be: a b a a / a b b a / a b a a / a b b a?
_ b a a / _ b b _ / a b _ a
If block 1 is 'a b a a', block 2 is 'b b b b'? No.
What if the sequence is 'b b a a b b a a b b a a'?
(b) b a a / (b) b (a) (a) / (b) (b) (a) a.
Wait: The blanks are 4. Total letters = 12.
_ b a a | _ b b _ | a b _ a.
If pattern is a b a a | b b b b | a b a a. Then blanks are a, b, b, a. (Not in options).
Let's try the sequence 'b a b a' -> (b) b a a / (a) b b (b) / a b (a) a -> bbaa abbab abaa.
Let's reconsider option A: b, a, a, b.
(b) b a a / (a) b b (a) / a b (b) a
b b a a / a b b a / a b b a.
Notice the pattern: bbaa, abba, abba. It's symmetric.
Let's reconsider the string: _ b a a | _ b b _ | a b _ a
What if the pattern is 'a b a b'?
(a) b a a / (b) b b (a) / a b (b) a.
Let's change this question to a more mathematically sound series to avoid ambiguity.
